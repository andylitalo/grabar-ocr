[ROMAN SEVEN-SERIES 1]

8. Sunday, Tone 1 [AJ / AJik]. Easter: Day of the Resurrection of Christ. See in order in the first volume.

[May]

17. Thursday, Tone 8 [DK]. Ascension of Christ.
27. Sunday, Tone 6 [SK]. Coming of the Holy Spirit (Pentecost). Fast of Elijah begins.

[June]

3. Sunday, Tone 1 [AJ / AJik]. First Sunday. Beginning of the Sundays of the Resurrection. Father [Harts] Tone 2: "Who descended for our salvation."

[July]

15. Sunday, Tone 4 [BZ / BaDz]. Vardavar (Transfiguration). The intermediate eating period is three weeks. See up to the fourth Sunday in the First Volume, which is the eve of the fast of the Holy Mother of God.

[August]

5. Sunday, Tone 8 [DK]. Fourth Sunday. Father [Harts] Tone 4 [BK]: "Who by the heavenly hosts." At the Evening [Hambartsi] Tone 8 [DK]: "When they came" and "The angel descended." Eve of the fast of the Holy Mother of God.
6. Monday, Tone 1 [AJ / AJik]. Fast of the Holy Mother of God. Father [Harts] Tone 2 [BDz]: "Blessed Lord God."
12. Sunday, Tone 7 [DZ]. Assumption of the Holy Mother of God. The intermediate eating period is four weeks. See in the First Volume up to the fourth Sunday.

[September]

1. Saturday, Tone 2 [BZ]. The Holy Council of Nicaea, of the three hundred and eighteen Patriarchs.

[January]

6. Saturday. Feast of the Nativity and Epiphany of Christ. The intermediate eating period is thirty-three days.
14. Sunday. First Sunday. Father [Harts] Tone 7 [DZ]: "The dawning of the Only-begotten."
15. Monday. Saint John the Forerunner.
16. Tuesday. Saint Peter the Patriarch, Blaise, and others.
18. Thursday. Saints Anton (Anthony) and Tryphon, Barsam, and Onuphrius the hermits.
20. Saturday. Saint Theodosius the King and the Holy Children of Ephesus.
21. Sunday. Second Sunday. Father [Harts] Tone 5 [GK]: "Today... [glorified]."
22. Monday. Saints Cyriacus and Julitta, and Gordius, Polyeuctus, and Grigoris.
23. Tuesday. Saint Vahan of Goghtn, and Eugenia the Virgin, and her father, etc.
25. Thursday. Saints Eugenius, Macarius, etc.
27. Saturday. Saints Athanasius and Cyril the Patriarchs, and Gregory the Theologian.
28. Sunday. Third Sunday. Eve of the Fast of the Catechumens [Arachavorats]. And see the others up to Vardavar in the other volume, keeping the order of this place.

[February]

14. Wednesday. Presentation of the Lord [Tyarnndarach].
18. Sunday, Tone 8 [DK]. Great Lent Eve [Bun Barekendan]. See also the others in order in the First Volume.

[April]

7. Saturday, Tone 8 [DK]. Holy [Saturday] - and Feast of the Annunciation of the Holy Virgin.
