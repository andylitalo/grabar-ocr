## English

ROMAN CALENDAR OF SEVEN-DAY WEEKS [Roman Directory]
January

6. Saturday. Feast of the Nativity and Theophany of Christ. The dispensation of eating meat [non-fasting period] is thirty days.
14. First Sunday. Tone 7 [dzk]: Canticle [Harts], "Rising of the Only-begotten" (Dagoomn miadzni).
15. Monday. Feast of John the Forerunner [John the Baptist].
16. Tuesday. Feast of Peter the Patriarch, Blaise, and the others.
18. Thursday. Feast of Anthony, and Triphon, Parsamas, and Onuphrius the solitary monks.
20. Saturday. Feast of Theodosius the King, and the Holy Infants of Ephesus.
21. First Tone 5 [gz]: Canticle [Harts], "Creator of all creatures" (Ararichd araradzots).
22. Monday. Feast of Cyriacus and Julitta, and of Gordius, Polyeuctus, and Grigoris.
23. Wednesday. Feast of Vahan of Goghtn, and of Eugine the Virgin, and the others.
25. Thursday. Feast of Eugenius, Macarius, and the others.
27. Saturday. Feast of the Holy Patriarchs Athanasius and Cyril, and of Gregory the Theologian.
28. Third Sunday. Tone 6 [gcl]: Canticle [Harts], "Today he was sacrificed" (Aysor zenav).
29. Monday. Feast of the Maccabees, Eleazar, and the others.
30. Tuesday. Feast of the Twelve Prophets: Hosea, and the others.

February

1. Thursday. Feast of Sophia and her three daughters.
3. Saturday. Feast of the Sons and Grandsons of the Enlightener [St. Gregory]. See these four feasts after the feast of Transfiguration [Vartavar]. But know this, that when a leap year occurs and the letter of the year is [letter code], do not celebrate these four feasts here, namely the Maccabees and the others, because they are celebrated on the last calendar date [letter code] after Transfiguration, as you see. Instead, celebrate on Monday the feast of Eugine the Virgin, on Saturday Gregory the Theologian, which were placed above with the others, and on Tuesday and Thursday Andrew the General and Arrianus, which are placed below after the Assumption of the Mother of God. But in other years, celebrate without doubt exactly as it is set down.
4. Fourth Sunday. Eve [Barigentan] of the Fast of the Catechumens [Arachavorats].
11. Sunday of Saint Sarkis.
14. Wednesday. Presentation of the Lord [Tyarnuntarach].
25. Sunday. Tone 8 [dkl]. Sunday of Great Lent [Boon Barigentan]. See also the others according to their order in the First Volume.

April

7. Saturday. Tone 1 [ahz]. The Raising of Lazarus, and the Feast of the Annunciation of the Holy Virgin.
15. Sunday. Tone 1 [az]. Easter [Zatik]: Day of the Resurrection of Christ. See according to the order in the First Volume.

May

24. Thursday. Tone 8 [dkl]. The Ascension of Christ. See according to the order in the First Volume.

June

3. Sunday. Tone 2 [akl]. The Coming of the Holy Spirit [Pentecost]. Eve [Barigentan] of the Elijah Fast.
10. First Sunday. Tone 1 [az]. The beginning of the Sundays of the Resurrection. Canticle [Harts] Tone 1 [adz], "Who came for our salvation" (Or yegir i prgootiun).
