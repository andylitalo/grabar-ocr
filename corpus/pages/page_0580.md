THE SEVEN-YEAR CYCLE OF THE ROMANS 1.
In January:

6. SATURDAY. Feast of the Nativity and Theophany of Christ. The fast-free period is fifteen days.
13. Saturday. Eighth day of the Nativity, and the Circumcision of Christ.
14. Sunday. First [Sunday]. Tone 3 [gd]: [Hymn for the] Father of the Only-begotten.
15. Monday. [Feast of] John the Forerunner.
16. Tuesday. [Feast of] Peter the Patriarch, Blaise, and others.
18. Thursday. [Feast of] Anthony, and Tryphon, Barsama, and Onuphrius the hermits.
20. Saturday. [Feast of] Theodosius the King, and the Holy Infants of Ephesus.
21. Sunday. Tone 7 [dz]. [Hymn for the] Father: Glorified. Barekendan [Eve] of the Fast of the Catechumens. And for the rest, see the First Volume up to Transfiguration [Vardavar], keeping the order of this place.

In February:
11. Sunday. Tone 8 [dk]. Great Barekendan [Eve of Great Lent].
14. Wednesday. Tone 4 [bz]. Presentation of the Lord [Tyarnndaraj]. See according to the order in the First Volume.

In April:
1. Sunday. Tone 1 [az]. Easter: Day of the Resurrection of Christ.
7. Saturday. Tone 7 [dz]. Feast of the Annunciation of the Holy Virgin Mary.

In May:
20. Sunday. Tone 2 [ak]. Coming of the Holy Spirit [Pentecost]. Barekendan of the Fast of Elijah.
27. Sunday. Tone 1 [az]. First [Sunday]. Beginning of the Sundays of the Resurrection. Tone 1 [adz]. [Hymn for the] Father: Who came for salvation.

In July:
8. Sunday. Tone 3 [bz]. Transfiguration [Vardavar]. The fast-free period is four weeks. See up to the Friday of the third week in the other volume; and on Saturday come here, and celebrate these feasts set down here, which are after the eight days of the Nativity. But if it is a leap year, and the letter of the year is 30 [L-I], celebrate here only [Gregory] the Theologian, because the other patriarchs were in the first letter 30 [L]. But in other years, as they are set down here, so shall you celebrate.
28. Saturday. Tone 7 [dz]. [Feast of] Athanasius and Cyril, and Gregory the Theologian.
29. Sunday. Tone 8 [dk]. Fourth [Sunday]. Tone 4 [bk]. [Hymn for the] Father: Who [reigns with] the heavenly hosts. Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 8 [dk]: When they came, and The Angel Descended.
30. Monday. Tone 1 [az]. [Feast of] Cyriacus and Julitta, and Gordius, Polyeuctus, and Grigoris. Tone 7 [dz]. [Hymn for the] Father: Holy art Thou.
31. Tuesday. Tone 2 [ak]. [Feast of] Vahan of Goghtn, and Eugenia the Virgin, and others. Tone 8 [dk]. [Hymn for the] Father: To You, O God.

4. Saturday. Tone 6 [gk]. [Feast of] the Holy Council of Ephesus of the two hundred patriarchs.
