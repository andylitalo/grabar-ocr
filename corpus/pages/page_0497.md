CANON OF THE COMING OF THE HOLY SPIRIT

On the Sunday of the Coming of the Holy Spirit, which is called Pentecost, that is, the fiftieth day from Pascha. 
At night there is a vigil, and they shall place the Holy Sacrament upon the table of preparation. 
Processional hymn (tone [gd]): "Our Lord ascended into heaven" (Hambartsaw tērn mer yerkins). 
Hymn of blessing [ah.] Tone 4 [dz]: "The dove-like apostle" (Arakeloy aghawnwoy). 
First Alleluia: "A clean heart..." "Cast me not..." 
Response: "The wings of a dove covered with silver..." "When the heavenly King scattered..." 
Hymn of the Fathers [Harts], Tone 1 [ah]: "The Coming of the Spirit" (Hogwoyn zgaloustn), with its variables. 
Morning Song: "Let us bless the Coming of the Spirit" (Zgaloust hogwoyn orhnescouk), "Bless the Lord in the churches" (Yekegeghetsis orhnetsek). 
Litany: "Let us all say" (Asascouk amen). 
"Holy God," who didst come and rest in the apostles. 
During this week, this Song, Litany, and "Holy God" are used.

Gospel according to John 14:15: "If you love me... [to verse 24]... who sent me." 
Litany: "For the peace from above" (Vasn i veroust). 
And they shall sign the four corners of the world with the Cross [Andastan]. 
Litany: "By the Holy Cross" (Sourb khachivs). 
Prayer: "Guardian" (Pahpanea). 
And then they shall begin the hymn of rest in the proper tone.

Then "Newly-created" (Norasteghtseal). 
Litany: "Let us ask" (Khndrescouk), "Save us" (Ketso).
