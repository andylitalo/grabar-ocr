## English

Risen. Eve of the Fast of the Holy Cross.

6. Monday. Tone 7 [Dikh / Dzajndzakh]. Fast of the Holy Cross. Canticle of the Steps [Harts]: "The captive children."

8. Wednesday. Tone 8 [Di / Dzajnakogh]. Fast. Feast of the Birth of the Holy Mother of God from Anna.

12. Sunday. Tone 8 [Di / Dzajnakogh]. Exaltation of the Holy Cross. Until the seventeenth Sunday, see the First Volume; but for the Canticles of the Steps [Harts] of the Martyrs and of the Fasts, the Resurrection Evening Hymns [Hambartsis], the Tone of the days, and the feasts of the last week, see the M Year-book, keeping the alphabetical sequence.

NOVEMBER

21. Sunday. Tone 1 [Ayp-Dza / Ajts]. Eleventh [Sunday]. Feast of the Presentation of the Holy Mother of God.
Eve of the Fast of the Advent [Yisnakats].

22. Monday. Tone 2 [Ayp-Ken / Ajts-Kogh]. Fast of the Advent [Yisnakats]. Canticle of the Steps [Harts] of the Resurrection: "God of our fathers, who in tribulation."

28. Sunday. Tone 8 [Di-Ken / Dzajnakogh-Kogh]. First [Sunday of Advent]. Canticle of the Steps [Harts] Tone 2 [Ayp-Ken / Ajts-Kogh]: "Beginningless Word." Evening Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 8 [Di-Ken / Dzajnakogh-Kogh]: "Thy angel, O Lord."

29. Monday. Tone 1 [Ayp-Dza / Ajts]. [Feast of Saints] Julian and Basilissa, and Indus and Domna, and the others. Canticle of the Steps [Harts] Tone 4 [Pen-Ken / Pets-Kogh]: "Those in battle." And for the others, see the First Volume, keeping the order of this place.

DECEMBER

5. Sunday. Tone 7 [Dikh / Dzajndzakh]. Second [Sunday of Advent]. Canticle of the Steps [Harts] Tone 3 [Pen-Dza / Pets]: "Incorruptible." Evening Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 7 [Dikh / Dzajndzakh]: "Thou who art above."

6. Monday. Tone 8 [Di-Ken / Dzajnakogh-Kogh]. [Feast of Saints] Gennadius and Mercurius, and James and Philemon.

7. Tuesday. Tone 1 [Ayp-Dza / Ajts]. [Feast of] the Egyptian Fathers: Paul, Poimen, and the others.

8. Wednesday. Tone 2 [Ayp-Ken / Ajts-Kogh]. Canticle of the Steps [Harts] of the Fast Tone 7 [Dikh / Dzajndzakh]: "Who the three children."

9. Thursday. Tone 3 [Pen-Dza / Pets]. Feast of the Conception of the Holy Virgin by Anna.

12. Sunday. Tone 6 [Kim-Ken / Kamts-Kogh]. Third [Sunday of Advent]. Canticle of the Steps [Harts] Tone 4 [Pen-Ken / Pets-Kogh]: "Beginningless God." Evening Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 6 [Kim-Ken / Kamts-Kogh]: "Having taken also ours." Eve of the Fast of Saint James.
