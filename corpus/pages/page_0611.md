## English

July

22. Sunday. Tone 2 [bch - Bk]. Vardavar [Transfiguration of Christ]. The post-feast period of eating meat is two weeks. See in the First Volume up to Friday of the second week; for Saturday come here and see.

August

4. Saturday. Tone 8 [dk - Gk]. Holy Ecumenical Council of Ephesus of the two hundred Church Fathers.

5. Sunday. Tone 1 [adz - Ayb-Dza]. Third [Sunday]. Canticle [Harts] Tone 4 [bk - Ben-Kog]: "Who from the heavenly hosts" (Vor yergnayin zinvoru). Song of the Ascent [Hambartsi] Tone 1 [adz - Ayb-Dza]: "Glory to Thy resurrection" (P'ark' harut'yan k'o). Eve [Barekendan] of the Fast of the Holy Mother of God.

6. Monday. Tone 2 [ak - Ayb-Kog]. Fast of the Holy Mother of God. Canticle [Harts] Tone 2 [ak - Ayb-Kog]: "Ineffable Essence" (Zandzar eut'iund).

12. Sunday. Tone 8 [dk - Gk]. Assumption [Dormition] of the Holy Mother of God. The post-feast period of eating meat is four weeks. See in the First Volume up to the fourth Sunday.

September

2. Sunday. Tone 5 [gch - Gim-Dza]. Fourth [Sunday]. Canticle [Harts] Tone 3 [bz - Ben-Dza]: "We glorify Thee" (P'aravoremk' zk'ez). Song of the Ascent [Hambartsi] Tone 5 [gch - Gim-Dza]: "The Myrrh-bearing women" (Yughaber) and "Today the resurrection" (Aysor harut'iun).

3. Monday. Tone 6 [gk - Gim-Kog]. Andrew the General, and his army, etc.

4. Tuesday. Tone 7 [dch - Da-Dza]. Adrian and his wife Anatolia, etc. Canticle [Harts] Tone 5 [gch - Gim-Dza]: 3rd [tone], "Hoping" (Yusats'yal).

6. Thursday. Tone 1 [adz - Ayb-Dza]. Abraham and Khoren, Cosmas and Damian, and Astuadzatur the Martyr. Canticle [Harts] Tone 7 [dz - Da-Dza]: "Holy art Thou, Lord" (Surb es ter).

8. Saturday. Tone 3 [bdz - Ben-Dza]. Feast of the Nativity of the Holy Mother of God from Anna.

9. Sunday. Tone 4 [bk - Ben-Kog]. Fifth [Sunday]. Canticle [Harts] Tone 6 [gk - Gim-Kog]: "Ineffable" (Andzareli). Song of the Ascent [Hambartsi] Tone 4 [bk - Ben-Kog]: "Christ arose" (K'ristos yareav). Eve [Barekendan] of the Fast of the Holy Cross.

10. Monday. Tone 5 [gdz - Gim-Dza]. Fast of the Holy Cross. Canticle [Harts] Tone 4 [bk - Ben-Kog]: "The captive youths" (Mankunk'n gerealk').

16. Sunday. Tone 3 [bdz - Ben-Dza]. Exaltation of the Holy Cross [Khachverats]. Until the tenth Sunday, see according to the order in the First Volume, keeping the order of this place.

Sunday. Tone 2 [ak - Ayb-Kog]. Second [Sunday]. Song of the Ascent [Hambartsi] Tone 2 [ak - Ayb-Kog]: "Risen" (Haruts'eal). Eve [Barekendan] of the Cross of Varag.

Monday. Tone 3 [bdz - Ben-Dza]. Fast. Mamas, etc. Canticle [Harts] Tone 8 [dk - Gk]: "To Thee, O God" (Ar k'ez astuadz).

Wednesday. Tone 5 [gch - Gim-Dza]. Canticle [Harts] Tone 5 [gch - Gim-Dza]: "Glorified is Thy name forever, O God" (P'aravoreal e anun k'o yavit'ean astuadz).

Sunday. Tone 1 [adz - Ayb-Dza]. Third [Sunday]. Feast of the Holy Cross of Varag. The post-feast period of eating meat is seven weeks.

October

Sunday. Tone 8 [dk - Gk]. Fourth [Sunday]. Song of the Ascent [Hambartsi] Tone 8 [dk - Gk]: "When they came" (Yorzham ekin), and "The angel descended" (Ej hreshtakn), and "The hosts of angels" (Hreshtakats'n dask').

Sunday. Tone 7 [dz - Da-Dza]. Fifth [Sunday]. Song of the Ascent [Hambartsi] Tone 5 [gdz - Gim-Dza]: "The women" (Kanants'n), and "Awesome" (Ahagin).

Sunday. Tone 6 [gk - Gim-Kog]. Sixth [Sunday]. Song of the Ascent [Hambartsi] Tone 6 [gk - Gim-Kog]: "The divine" (Zastuadzayin), and "Thy resurrection" (Zk'o harut'iund).

Sunday. Tone 5 [gdz - Gim-Dza]. Seventh [Sunday]. Discovery of the Holy Cross [Gyut Khach].

Wednesday. Tone 7 [dz - Da-Dza]. The Hyperichians and Severianos, etc. Canticle [Harts] Tone 5 [gch - Gim-Dza]: "Holy art Thou, Lord of hosts" (Surb es ter zorut'eants').

Wednesday. Tone 8 [dk - Gk]. Canticle [Harts] Tone 6 [gk - Gim-Kog]: "Holy God, Father without beginning" (Surb astuadz hayr anskizbn). And the others according to the order in the First Volume.

November

Saturday. Tone 3 [bdz - Ben-Dza]. All Saints, ancient and new, etc.

Sunday. Tone 4 [bk - Ben-Kog]. Eighth [Sunday]. Song of the Ascent [Hambartsi] Tone 4 [bk - Ben-Kog]: "In the morning the holy women" (Aravotean surb kanayk'n).

Sunday. Tone 3 [bdz - Ben-Dza]. Ninth [Sunday]. Song of the Ascent [Hambartsi] Tone 3 [bdz - Ben-Dza]: "To the tomb" (I gerezman), to the end.

Sunday. Tone 2 [ak - Ayb-Kog]. Tenth [Sunday]. Song of the Ascent [Hambartsi] Tone 2 [ak - Ayb-Kog]: "The wondrous angel" (Sk'ancheli hreshtakn), to the end. Eve [Barekendan] of the Fast of the Advent [Yisnak].

Monday. Tone 3 [bdz - Ben-Dza]. Fast of Advent. Canticle [Harts] Tone 7 [dz - Da-Dza]: "God of our fathers, who in affliction" (Harts'n merots' astuadz vor i neghut'ean).

Wednesday. Tone 5 [gdz - Gim-Dza]. Fast day. Feast of the Presentation of the Holy Mother of God.

Sunday. Tone 1 [adz - Ayb-Dza]. First [Sunday of Advent]. Canticle [Harts] Tone 3 [bdz - Ben-Dza]: "Incorruptible Word" (Anapakan band). Song of the Ascent [Hambartsi] Tone 1 [adz - Ayb-Dza]: "With the women" (End kanaysn), and "Who ineffably" (Vor andzar).

Monday. Tone 2 [ak - Ayb-Kog]. Julian and Basilissa. Canticle [Harts] Tone 3 [bdz - Ben-Dza]: "Without malice" (Anokhakal). See according to the order in the First Volume.
