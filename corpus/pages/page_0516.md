## English

Prayer, "Save [us]" (Pahpanea). And then "Bow down" (Khonarhëtso) and "Live" (Apretso). Responsorial [Mesedi] of the Resurrection, "The Lord reigns, let [the earth] rejoice" (Ter tagavoreats tsn.). "They appeared" (Erevetsan). Litany, "Let us say" (Asastsook). "Holy God, who wast revealed on Mount Tabor." Hymn of Ascent [Hambartsi], Tone 8 [dk], "Today on the mountain" (Aysor i lerinn). After "Father of Compassions" (Hayr gtatssin), Psalm 22 [23], "The Lord shall shepherd" (Ter hovvestse). Book of Exodus 24:12, "And the Lord said... to verse 18, on the mountain." Book of 3 Kings [1 Kings] 19:11, "And the Lord said to Elijah... to verse 16, in your place." Prophet Isaiah 40:9, "Go up onto a high mountain... to verse 17, were counted to him." Apostle, Hebrews 12:18, "For you have not approached... to verse 27, those things which cannot be shaken may remain." Alleluia, "Yours are the heavens." Gospel according to Luke 9:18, "And it came to pass, as he was alone... to verse 36, of those things which they had seen." And then, Prayer of Saint Elishe the Vardapet, "Blessed art thou, Lord God." And at the end, Litany, "Let us ask" (Khndrestsook). "Save [us]" (Ketso). Prayer, "King of Peace."

On the Second Day: Tone 4 [ah. bk], "Ray of glory" (Tshatshagayt parats), with its canon [sarokn]. Magnificat [Mets.], "Mother of God, higher than the cherubim." Song, "Yours are the heavens." Litany, "Let us say." Canticle of the youths [Mkk.], Tone 4 [bk], "Bless the Lord, children." After the youth canticle [Mankuntsn], Litany, "For peace." Prayer, "Holy art thou, Lord." Psalm 98 [99], "Exalt [the Lord our God]." Book of Exodus 19:9, "And the Lord said to Moses... to verse 13, they on the mountain." Book of 4 Kings [2 Kings] 2:9, "And it came to pass, when they had crossed... to verse 12, he saw him." Catholic Epistle, 1 John 5:9, "Brethren, if we receive the witness of men... to verse 12, and does not have life." Alleluia, "The heights." Gospel according to John 1:1, "In the beginning was... to verse 17, came through Christ." Song, "Creator" (Ararchakan). Litany, "Let us ask." "Save [us]" (Kketso). Prayer, "To your almighty."
Midday Introit: "The Lord appears today." Antiphon, "The Lord reigns." Hymn, Tone 4 [bk], "Ray [of glory]." Psalm 98 [99], "Exalt [the Lord our God]." The Scripture readings and Gospel are the same as the morning's. Eucharistic Hymn [Srbasatsutyun]: "Angelic" (Hreshtakayin).
In the Evening: Responsorial [Mese.], "Sing to the Lord." "Enter." Hymn of Ascent [Hmb.], Tone 4 [bk], "Bless the Lord, children." Litany, "Let us ask." "Save [us]." Prayer, "King of Peace."

On the Third Day: Tone 5 [ah. g3], "Today he who is" (Aysor vor enn), with its canon. Magnificat [Mets.], "Bright Mother of God." Song, "Yours are the heavens." Litany, "Let us say." Canticle of the youths [Mkk.], Tone 8 [dk], poetic style [steghi], "All mountains." After the youth canticle [Mankuntsn], "For peace." Prayer, "Holy art thou, Lord." Psalm 132 [133], "Like the dew that falls... Antiphon, For it is good." Book of Exodus 19:16, "And it came to pass on the day... to verse 20, there Moses." Book of 4 Kings [2 Kings] 19:11 [or 3 Kings 19:11], "And the Lord said to Elijah... to verse 16, in your place." Apostle, Hebrews 12:18, "For you have not approached... to verse 27, those things which cannot be shaken may remain." Alleluia, "A bright cloud." Gospel according to Luke 9:27, "But truly... to verse 36, of those things which they had seen." Song, "Essential" (Eakan). After: Litany, "Let us ask." "Save [us]." Prayer, "To your almighty."
Midday Introit: "The Lord appears." Hymn, Tone 5 [g3], "Today he who is." Psalm, Scripture readings, and Gospel are the same as the morning's. Eucharistic Hymn [Srbasatsutyun]: "Angelic."
In the Evening: Responsorial [Mese.], "Come and see." "Our God." Hymn of Ascent [Hmb.], Tone 8 [dk], poetic style [steghi], "All mountains." After, Litany, "Let us ask." "Save [us]." Prayer, "King of Peace."

Wednesday: Tone 6 [gk]. Father [Harts], Tone 2 [ak], "Blessed and glorified." Midday Psalm 64 [65], "Save your servants." Apostle, 1 Corinthians 1:1, "Paul, called... to verse 6, of Jesus Christ." Alleluia, "Have mercy on me, Lord." Gospel according to Matthew 17:21 [17:22], "And while they were gathering... to chapter 18, verse 4, in the kingdom of heaven."
