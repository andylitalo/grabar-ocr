## English

[On the Sabbath]:
Song: "The Lord after creation" (Tērn yet steghtsmann). Litany: "Let us ask" (Kndrests`uk`). Prayer: "To Your all-powerful and wondrous burial." And then, Hymn Tone 2 [bka], "Your Cross, O Christ" (Khach`i k`o K`ristos), according to the canon of the Evening: "We magnify Your holy crucifixion, and we glorify Your holy burial." Likewise: "Glory to Your holy Cross, Alleluia; to Your crucifixion, Alleluia; to Your holy burial, Alleluia." And they shall kiss the holy Cross and the Gospel book. Litany: "By the Holy Cross." Prayer: "Protect us" (Pahpanya).

And on the Evening of the Vigil of Holy Easter, at the ninth hour, they shall assemble in the holy church, keeping the altar veiled. And they shall light three lamps, and they shall say the three "Have mercy on us" (Vogharmyasn). But "Bow down" (Khonarh`etso) and "Live" (Aprets`o) are not said. Psalm 112 [113], chanted from the right side choir: "Blessed be the name of the Lord." Response: "Praise, O children" (Orhnets`ek` mankunk`). After this, Hymn Tone 7 [yi3]: "Today divinity rejoices." And at its completion: "Again and again in peace," "Blessing and..." Again, Psalm 117 [118]: "This is the day which the Lord has made." Response: "Give thanks to the Lord."
Book of Genesis 1:1: "In the beginning God created..." to verse 3:24: "...the tree of life." Prayer with genuflection, [saying] "Lord, have mercy" three times.
After this: "Again and again in peace," "Blessing and..."
Book of Genesis 22:1: "And it came to pass after these things..." to verse 18: "...you have obeyed my voice." Prayer with genuflection.
Book of Exodus 12:1: "And the Lord spoke..." to verse 24: "...forever." Prayer with genuflection.
Prophet Jonah 1:1: "Now the word of the Lord came..." to verse 4:11: "...and also much cattle." Prayer with genuflection.
Book of Exodus 14:24: "And it came to pass in the morning..." to verse 15:21: "...he has cast into the sea."

And then they shall light torches and lamps, and they shall give candles into the hands of everyone; and coming together into the chancel, they shall begin the Hymn Tone 8 [ē3]: "Shine, Jerusalem." After this, Prayer with [genuflection].
Prophet Isaiah 60:1: "Shine..." to verse 13: "...I will make glorious." Prayer with genuflection.
Book of Job 38:1: "Who is this that darkens..." to verse 28: "...begotten in the heavens." Prayer with genuflection.
Book of Joshua 1:1: "Now it came to pass after the death..." to verse 9: "...whithersoever you go." Prayer with genuflection.
Book of 3 Kings [1 Kings] 2:1: "And it came to pass, when [the Lord] would take up..." to verse 22: "...which he spoke." Prayer with genuflection.
Prophet Jeremiah 31:31: "Behold, the days come..." to verse 34: "...I will remember no more." Prayer with genuflection.
Prophet Ezekiel 37:1: "The hand of the Lord was upon me..." to verse 14: "...says the Lord Lord." Prayer with genuflection.

And then one shall go up to the altar stage, and upon the altar stage he shall read the Book of Daniel 3:1: "In the eighteenth year..." And when he comes to that verse where it says: "Bless the Lord, all works of the Lord," the clerks shall begin to sing in a sweet voice: "Bless, praise, and highly exalt him forever," to verse 90: "...his mercy." And at the completion, they shall open the altar, and the deacon shall proclaim in a loud voice: "Bless, Lord," and the celebrant [shall say]: "Blessed is the kingdom of the Father and of the Son."

Midday Liturgy Introit: "Christ is risen from the dead." Response: "Praise the Lord, O Jerusalem." Hymn Tone 1 [ats]: "Christ is risen from the dead." After this, the deacon shall call out: "Let us attend" (Proskhume); and they shall begin to say the great "Holy God" (Surb Astvats) with "who rose from the dead." Psalm link: "To You is due praise." Epistle: 1 Corinthians 15:1: "Now I make known unto you..." to verse 11: "...so you believed." Alleluia: "God arose." Gospel: Matthew 28:1: "In the end of the Sabbath..." to verse 20: "...to the end of the world." And then: "We believe in one God." Eucharistic Hymn (Srbasats`ut`iwn): "Who is like the Lord?"

And after the dismissal of the Liturgy, they shall begin to perform the Pre-festal service (Nakhatonak) without fail. Hymn Tone 1 [ats]: "Today He arose," with its proper additions. After this, Litany: "Let us ask," "Save us." Prayer: "King of peace."

And then, proceeding to the table with the Cross and Gospel book, they shall begin the Hymn Tone 1 [ats]: "Today He arose." And at its completion: "Stand up" (Ort`iw). Gospel: Matthew 28:16: "Then the eleven disciples..." to verse 20: "...to the end of the world." Litany: "By the Holy Cross." Prayer: "Protect us" (Pahpanya). Our Father.
