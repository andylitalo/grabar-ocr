## English

But if it happens to fall during the Fast of Catechumens [Yarajavorats],
or during the Great Lent, on February 13, at the Midday Hour:
they shall say the three "Have Mercy" [Oghormya] hymns according to the order of the day,
and they shall read the Scripture [books] of the day;
they shall say the "For the Souls" [Hogvots] prayer and be dismissed,
omitting [the prayers] "Bow Down [Your Ear]" (Khonarhetso) and "Save [Us]" (Apretso).
Then in the evening, "Bow Down [Your Ear]" (Khonarhetso) and "Save [Us]" (Apretso) and so on, according to the above-written instruction.
And during the night, which is February 14,
there is a vigil with services and psalms.
And the Holy Sacrament is placed upon a table in the chancel;
and all, having vested, shall come into the chancel,
and they shall say the processional [hetevak] hymn in Tone 3 [gd]: "Who became incarnate from the Virgin" (Marmnatsyald i kusen).
And the Blessing [Orhnutiun] hymn in Tone 4 [dad]: "Today, without beginning" (Aysor anskzbnakan).
Alleluia. "Blessed is He who comes" and "The Lord our God."
And then they shall read the four Gospels in the four corners of the church.

The first Gospel they shall read facing the East.
Hymn, Tone 1 [ats]: "Who became incarnate" (Marmnatsyald), "Rejoice greatly" (Urakh ler yoyzh), "Image-sharing" (Patkerakits).
After which: "Stand [firm]" (Ortiu). Gospel according to Matthew 5:17–20: "Do not think that..." to "...into the kingdom of heaven."

The second facing the West side.
Hymn, Tone 4 [dz]: "Today, without beginning" (Aysor anskzbnakan), "Today having come to the temple" (Aysor i tacharn ekial).
After which: "Stand [firm]" (Ortiu). Gospel according to Mark 13:32–37: "But about that day..." to "...keep watch."

The third facing the South side.
Hymn, Tone 4 [dz]: "Light from Light" (Loys i lusoy), "You who [by] Spirit" (Zkez vor hogi).
After which: "Stand [firm]" (Ortiu). Gospel according to Luke 12:49–59: "I came to bring fire..." to "...the very last penny."

And the fourth facing the North side.
Hymn, Tone 4 [ek]: "Today in the temple" (Aysor i tacharn), "In the beginning was the Word" (I skzbane bann).
After which: "Stand [firm]" (Ortiu). Gospel according to John 1:1–17: "In the beginning was the Word..." to "...came through Christ."

After which, hymn, Tone 2 [bk]: "At the coming of the Lord" (I galstean tearn).
And then coming into the chancel they shall say the "Fathers" [Harts] hymn with its canons.
And if it happens on a Sunday,
read the Myrrhbearers [Yughaberits] Gospel.
Song: "Sun of Justice" (Aregakn ardarutean).
Litany: "Holy Mother" (Mayr surb). "Holy God."
