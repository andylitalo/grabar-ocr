## English

19. Sunday. Tone 3 [GDz]. Fourth [Sunday]. Canticle of the Fathers [Harts] Tone 6 [Gk] - "Glorified Son" (Paravorial vorti). Gospel [Hambartsi] Tone 3 [GDz] - "The oil-bearing [women]" (Yughaber) to the end.
20. Monday. Tone 6 [Gk]. [Feast of] Ignatius and Addai, Patriarchs, and Maruthas, and Theopompus and Theonas, Bishops, and the others.
21. Tuesday. Tone 7 [Dz]. [Feast of] Abgar, the King. The Scripture readings and Gospel are those of Theodosius.
23. Thursday. Tone 1 [Az]. [Feast of] David the Prophet and James the Apostle.
26. Sunday. Tone 2 [Bk]. Fifth [Sunday]. Canticle of the Fathers [Harts] Tone 6 [Gk] - "Before eternity" (Nakhahavityan). Gospel [Hambartsi] Tone 2 [Bk] - "They anticipated [the dawn]" (Kanthechetsin) to the end.
29. Wednesday. Tone 7 [Dz]. Canticle of the Fathers [Harts] Tone 8 [Dk] - "Blessed art thou, Lord God of our fathers." Barekendan [eve] of the Fast of the Nativity.

January.
2. Sunday. Tone 2 [Bz]. Sixth [Sunday] of the Fast. Gospel [Hambartsi] Tone 2 [Bz] - "Today [the angels] rejoice" (Aysor zvartcha) to the end.
3. Monday. Tone 2 [Bk]. Fast day. Canticle of the Fathers [Harts] Tone 8 [Dk] - "Blessed art thou, Lord God of our fathers."
5. Wednesday. Tone 6 [Gk]. Eve of the Nativity of Christ.

Dominical Letter of the Romans is 4.
January.
6. TUESDAY. Feast of the Nativity and Epiphany of Christ. The period of non-fasting [eating days] is twenty-seven days.
15. Thursday. [Feast of] John the Forerunner.
17. Saturday. [Feast of] Peter the Patriarch, Blaise, and the others.
18. Sunday. First [Sunday after Epiphany]. Canticle of the Fathers [Harts] Tone 7 [Dz] - "The dawning of the Only-begotten" (Tsagumn miatsni).
19. Monday. [Feast of] Antony, and Tryphon, Barsama, and Onuphrius, the hermits.
20. Tuesday. [Feast of] Theodosius the King, and the Holy Infants of Ephesus.
22. Thursday. [Feast of] Cyriacus and Julietta, and Gordius, Polyeuctus, and Gregory.
24. Saturday. [Feast of] Athanasius and Cyril, and Gregory the Theologian.
25. Sunday. Second [Sunday after Epiphany]. Canticle of the Fathers [Harts] Tone 6 [Gk] - "Today He was born" (Aysor tsenav).
26. Monday. [Feast of] Vahan of Goghtn, and Eugenia the Virgin, and the others.
27. Tuesday. [Feast of] Eugenius, Macarius, etc., and Andrew the Commander and his army, and the others.
29. Thursday. [Feast of] Adrian and his wife Natalia, and the others. But know that when it is a leap year, and the letter of the year is N3, during this period do not celebrate Andrew the Commander and Adrian, because their feast is on the final letter Y, on the tenth Sunday. Instead, on Monday celebrate only Vahan, on Tuesday Eugenia the Virgin, and on Thursday Eugenius, Macarius, and the others. But in other years, celebrate without fail exactly as written here.
31. Saturday. [Feast of] Isaac the Parthian, our great Patriarch. For on the day of his feast is the Presentation of the Lord [Candlemas].

February.
1. Sunday. Third [Sunday after Epiphany]. Barekendan of the Fast of Catechumens [Arachtchavorats].
8. Sunday. [Feast of] Saint Sarkis.
14. Saturday. Presentation of the Lord [Tyarnndaraj].
