THE SEVEN-YEAR CYCLE OF THE ROMANS 1.
However, when it is a leap year, and
the year-letter is 208 [ԸծՏ], do not celebrate
these four feasts, namely: Cyprian, Athanagenes,
the Forefathers, and Thaddeus the Apostle,
at this time. For their feasts are celebrated
in the last year-letter, 300 [Տ], after Transfiguration,
as you see. But in place of Cyprian, celebrate Eugene the Virgin,
and in place of Thaddeus, [celebrate] Gregory the
Theologian, who was placed above with the other
feasts; and in place of Athanagenes
and the Forefathers, celebrate Andrew the Commander
and Adrian, who
are placed below after the Mother of God.
But in other years, as it is written,
so celebrate without doubt.
