## English

And after the Liturgy, they shall go to the water with Cross and Gospel, singing the hymn in Tone 3 [gd]: "Light from Light" (Loys i lue). After this: Psalm 28 [29], antiphon: "The voice of the Lord is upon [the waters]", response: "Ascribe to the Lord". And they shall read the scriptures:
The Prophet Isaiah 12:3, "Thus says the Lord: and you shall draw..." to verse 6, "...in the midst of them."
The Book of Joshua 3:14, "And it came to pass when [the people] set out..." to verse 17, "...passed over on dry ground."
The Book of Exodus 15:22, "And [Moses] led..." to verse 26, "...and he tested them."
The Book of Fourth Kings [2 Kings] 2:19, "And the men of the city said..." to verse 22, "...which he spoke."
The Prophet Ezekiel 47:1, "And he brought me..." to verse 12, "...and their outlet for healing."
The Apostle: 1 Corinthians 10:1, "I do not want [you to be ignorant]..." to verse 4, "...was Christ himself."
Alleluia: "Let the heavens rejoice."
Gospel: Matthew 3:1, "In those days..." to verse 17, "...with whom I am well pleased."
And the deacon proclaims what is in the directory. And they shall read the prayers of Saint Basil; they shall say the great litany aloud, and the other chants; they shall place only one Cross into the water. And the bishop, taking the holy sign [the Cross] and the holy Muron in hand, shall sign the water, saying: "May this water be blessed and sanctified by the sign of this holy Cross, and by this holy Muron, and by this holy Gospel, etc.", repeating this thrice. And then, taking the Cross out of the water, they shall sing the hymn in Tone 4 [dz]: "The Dove Sent Forth" (Arakeloy aghavnoy). And the bishop shall pour the holy Muron into the water.
Litany: "By this holy Cross." Prayer: "Guard us." "Our Father who art in heaven."

And they shall go into the church and perform the Pre-festal service of John the Baptist. Hymn, Tone 1 [ak]: "The Sun" (Aregakann) (and if they have gone outside, they shall sing it as they return). And at the start of the "Fathers" [hymn], everyone shall kiss the holy Gospel. And at the completion of the Pre-festal service: "Christ our God, for the sake of the holy Cross." Litany: "By the holy ascetics." Prayer: "You who chose and received."

During these eight days, the morning song is "Sun of righteousness." Litany: "Holy Mother of God." "Holy God... who wast born and didst reveal thyself." And after the "Holy God", the Psalm of the Gospel: Psalm 109 [110], antiphon: "In the beauty [of holiness]", response: "The Lord said to my Lord." And the Midday introits are three; say one of them each day.
In the Evening: "Bow down [Your ear]" and "Save [me]."
The Gradual Psalms (Mesedi) are three; say one of them each day:
a. "The Lord said to me." b. "He shall come down like rain." c. "He shall magnify." Litany: "Holy Mother of God." For the "Hambartsi" [hymn], say the Blessing hymn of the following day. But after "Compassionate Father", they shall go out together to the chancel with Cross and Gospel, incense and candles, and performing the Pre-festal service of the coming day with its proper "Fathers" hymn, they shall do so during these eight days. Litany: "Holy Mother of God." Prayer: "Receive, Lord, our supplications."

On the Second Day in the morning, Tone 2 [ahg]: "Today the Word from the Father", with its proper service. Alleluia of the Nativity. Psalm 109 [110], antiphon: "In the beauty [of holiness]", response: "The Lord said to my Lord." Gospel: Mark 1:1, "The beginning of the gospel..." to verse 13, "...and [the angels] ministered to him." During these eight days, say the "Ararchakan" [hymn] according to the proper day. Litany: "Let us ask." Prayer: "To You, O Lord." Midday Introit: "We beseech you." Psalm 5 [6], antiphon: "Lord, as with a shield", response: "Give ear to my words." Acts of the Apostles 6:8, "[And] Stephen..." to chapter 8:2, "...great lamentation over him." Alleluia: "Strengthened by the Holy Spirit." Gospel: John 12:24, "Truly, truly, I say to you..." to verse 26, "...my Father."

On the Third Day in the morning, Tone 2 [ahg]: "Co-essential", with its proper service. Psalm, antiphon: Psalm 109 [110], antiphon: "In the beauty [of holiness]", response: "The Lord said to my Lord." Gospel: Matthew 3:1, "In those days..." to verse 11, [or rather, Matthew 4] "...and they ministered to him." Midday Introit: "Mother of God, unwedded." Psalm 2, antiphon: "The Lord said to me", response: "Why did the heathen rage?" Epistle: Hebrews 1:1, "In many parts..." to verse 12, "...and your years shall not fail." Alleluia: "John the Forerunner." Gospel: Matthew 2:13, "And when they departed..." to verse 23, "...he shall be called a Nazarene."

On the Fourth Day in the morning, Tone 3 [ahf]: "Today Gabriel", with its proper service. Gospel: John 1:1, "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was..." to verse 17, "...came through Jesus Christ."
