## English

Monday. Tone 2 [g-k]. Of the Holy martyrs Callistratus, and the forty-nine martyrs, and Lucian the Priest. Night Office [ah.] of the Martyrs. Canticle of the Council [Harts] Tone 6 [b]: "With the saints." Midday Liturgy: Psalm 115:15 [116:15] "Precious." Proverbs 1:1 "To know wisdom" to verse 9 "on your neck." Prophet Isaiah 41:1 "Renew" to verse 3 "of his feet." Epistle Hebrews 12:1 "Wherefore seeing we also" to verse 7 "God [deals] with you." Alleluia of the Martyrs, from the tone. Gospel Mark 14:9 "And you indeed" to verse 13 "he shall live."

Tuesday. Tone 7 [d-3]. Feast of the Holy Prophet Zechariah. Night Office [ah.] Tone 2 [a-k] "Who the divine power." Canticle of the Council [Harts] Tone 1 [a-dz] "Who in the heavenly," with its canon. Magnificat [Mets.] "Revealed to you." Morning Canticle [Mkk.] Tone 6 [g-3] "Who beforehand." Midday Liturgy: Psalm 67:26 [68:26] "In the churches." Prophet Zechariah 3:7 "Thus says the Lord" to 4:9 "me to you." The death of Zechariah; this is not in the Bible, but is taken from the Synaxarion [Yaysmavourk]; see May 15. Epistle 1 Corinthians 12:28 "And those whom God appointed" to 13:3 "profiteth me nothing." Alleluia: "In the churches bless." Gospel Matthew 23:34 "Therefore" to verse 39 "in the name of the Lord."

Wednesday. Tone 6 [g-k]. Canticle of the Council [Harts] Tone 1 [a-3] "Blessed are you, O King." Midday Liturgy: Psalm 85 [86] "Save your servant." Epistle Romans 10:5 "Moses writes" to verse 17 "by the word of Christ." Alleluia: "Lord, your mercy." Gospel Matthew 13:31 "Another parable he put forth to them" to verse 42 "of teeth."

Thursday. Tone 1 [a-dz]. Feast of the Holy Prophet Elisha. Night Office [ah.] Tone 2 [a-k] "Who the divine." Canticle of the Council [Harts] Tone 6 [g-3] "Who with the earthly." Magnificat [Mets.] "Who in the heights." Morning Canticle [Mkk.] Tone 8 [d-k] "Today rejoices." Evening Litany [Hmb.] Tone 7 [d-dz] "Who beforehand." Midday Introit: "Lord, who established." Hymn [Sharakan] Tone 1 [a-dz] "Beam of light." Psalm 67:26 [68:26] "In the churches." Book of 4 Kings [2 Kings] 13:14 "And Elisha fell sick" to verse 21 "of his feet." Epistle 1 Corinthians 2:6 "Wisdom" to verse 10 "the depths of God." Alleluia: "In the churches." Gospel Luke 4:25 "Truly I say" to verse 30 "he went through their midst."

Friday. Tone 2 [a-k]. Canticle of the Council [Harts] of Rest, Tone 6 [g-k] "God the Word." Midday Liturgy: Psalm 97 [98] "They saw." Epistle Romans 11:1 "Now I say then" to verse 12 "their fullness." Alleluia: "Lord, rebuke me not in your anger." Gospel Matthew 13:43 "Then shall the righteous" to verse 52 "things new and old."

Saturday. Tone 3 [b-3]. Feast of the Holy Twelve Apostles of Christ, and of Saint Paul the thirteenth Apostle. Night Office [ah.] Tone 6 [g-3] "Blessed," with its canon. Morning Canticle [Mkk.] Tone 8 [d-k] "Apostles of God." Midday Introit: "Apostles." Hymn [Sharakan] Tone 2 [a-k] "In the infinite." Psalm 18:4 [19:4] "Through all the earth." Proverbs Wisdom 9:9 "And with you is wisdom" to 10:2 "to hold all things." Book of Exodus 15:23 "And the children came" to verse 27 "by the water." Prophet Hosea: "And the Lord shall come." (Note: This is not in the Bible, see the Lectionary [Chashots]). Epistle 1 Thessalonians 2:9 "Remember, brethren" to verse 16 "to the uttermost." Alleluia: "You shall make them." Gospel Matthew 9:36 "And seeing" to 10:7 "the kingdom of heaven." In the Evening: Evening Litany [Hambartsi] Tone 1 [a-dz] "Holy are you, O Lord" and "From you we entreat."

Sixth Sunday after Pentecost. Tone 4 [b-k]. Eve of the Fast of the Transfiguration [Vardavar], always. Canticle of the Council [Harts] Tone 2 [a-k] "Who for our salvation." Magnificat [Mets.] Tone 2 [a-k] "Of your new tabernacle." Midday Liturgy: Psalm 64 [65] "Praise is due to you." Prophet Isaiah 3:1 "Behold, the Lord" to verse 11 "shall pass through it." Epistle Romans 11:13 "But I speak to you" to verse 24 "into the olive tree." Alleluia: "Arise, Lord, in your command." Gospel Matthew 14:13 "And when he heard" to verse 21 "and children." In the Evening: Scripture [Mesedi] "Sing," "Enter." Evening Litany [Hamb.] Tone 4 [b-k] "Of your resurrection." And then the Fast of the Transfiguration [Vardavar] begins, but no feasts are held.

Monday. Tone 3 [b-3]. Canticle of the Council [Harts] Tone 1 [a-3] "Blessed are you forever." Midday Liturgy: Psalm 144 [145] "Your kingdom." Epistle Romans 11:25 "I would not, brethren" to verse 36 "glory forever, Amen." Alleluia: "Come, let us rejoice." Gospel Matthew 14:22 "And he constrained" to verse 36 "they were saved."
