April

June

17. Sunday, Tone 1 [aձ/ԱՁ]. First [Sunday]. Beginning of the Sundays of the Resurrection. See the Canticles of the Fathers [Harts] of the Sundays here.

July

8. Sunday, Tone 7 [gկ/ԳԿ]. Fourth [Sunday]. Canticle of the Fathers [Harts] Tone 1 [aձ]: "Who came for salvation" (Vor yekir i prkutyun).
Sunday, Tone 6 [bկ/ԲԿ]. Sixth [Sunday]. Canticle of the Fathers [Harts] Tone 2 [bձ]: "Who descended from heaven" (Vor i yernkits ijer). Day of abstinence for the Fast of Vardavar [Transfiguration].
Sunday, Tone 2 [bձ/ԲՁ]. Vardavar [Feast of the Transfiguration]. The period of non-fasting is one week. And see the rest according to the order.

August

Saturday, Tone 1 [aձ/ԱՁ]. The Holy Ecumenical Council of Ephesus of the two hundred Patriarchs.
Sunday, Tone 1 [aկ/ԱԿ]. Second [Sunday]. Canticle of the Fathers [Harts] Tone 2 [bձ]: "Who in the heavenly hosts" (Vor yerknayin zinvorutyantsd). Day of abstinence for the Fast of the Holy Mother of God [Asdvadzatsin].
Monday, Tone 2 [bձ/ԲՁ]. Fast day. Canticle of the Fathers [Harts] of Penitence, Tone 1 [aկ]: "Glorified forever" (Paravoryal yavedyan).
Sunday, Tone 1 [aձ/ԱՁ]. Assumption [Verapokhum] of the Holy Mother of God. The period of non-fasting is four weeks. See according to the order in the First Volume up to the fourth Sunday, keeping the order of the day.

September

Sunday, Tone 7 [gկ/ԳԿ]. Fourth [Sunday]. Canticle of the Fathers [Harts] Tone 2 [bձ]: "We glorify You" (Paravoremp zkyez). Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 7 [gկ]: "The Divine" (Zastvadsayin).
Monday, Tone 3 [gձ/ԳՁ]. Saint Andrew the Commander and his army, etc.
Tuesday, Tone 8 [dկ/ԴԿ]. Saint Adrian [Hadrian] and his wife Anatolia, etc. Canticle of the Fathers [Harts] Tone 6 [bկ]: "Those in battle" (Vork i mardi).
Wednesday, Tone 1 [aձ/ԱՁ]. Canticle of the Fathers [Harts] of Penitence, Tone 6 [bկ]: "Blessed art thou, Lord, God of our fathers" (Hartsən merots orhnyal es der).
Thursday, Tone 1 [aկ/ԱԿ]. Saint Abraham and Saint Khoren, Saint Cosmas and Saint Damian, and Saint Asdvadzadoor [Adeodatus] the Witness. Canticle of the Fathers [Harts] Tone 2 [bձ]: "Who the torments" (Vork ztanjans).
Saturday, Tone 6 [bկ/ԲԿ]. Feast of the Nativity of the Holy Mother of God from Anna.
Sunday, Tone 3 [gձ/ԳՁ]. Fifth [Sunday]. Canticle of the Fathers [Harts] Tone 7 [gկ]: "Ineffable" (Anchareli). Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 3 [gձ]: "The oil-bearing women" (Yughaber) and "Today the Resurrection" (Aysor yarutyun). Day of abstinence for the Holy Cross.
Monday, Tone 7 [gկ/ԳԿ]. Fast of the Holy Cross. Canticle of the Fathers [Harts] Tone 6 [bկ]: "God of our fathers" (Vor hartsən merots astvads).
Sunday, Tone 6 [bկ/ԲԿ]. Exaltation of the Holy Cross [Khachverats]. See up to the tenth Sunday in the First Volume, keeping the order of the day.
