## English

THE SEVEN-DAY CYCLES OF THE ROMANS 4.

In January:

6. Tuesday. Tone 2 [BK - B-flat / Second Side]. Feast of the Nativity and Theophany of Christ our God. The intervening period of eating is forty-one days.

14. Wednesday. Tone 2 [BK - B-flat / Second Side]. Fast. Penitential Father [Harts] Tone 1 [A-dz - A / First Class]: "The youths sang in the furnace and said" (Yergeyin mankunkn i hnocin yev asyein).

15. Thursday. Tone 3 [GD - G / Third Class]. Feast of the Birth of John the Forerunner [Precursor].

17. Saturday. Tone 4 [D-dz - D / Fourth Class]. [Feast of] Peter the Patriarch, Blaise the Bishop, and Abisalom the Deacon.

18. Sunday. Tone 4 [DK - D-flat / Fourth Side]. First [Sunday]. Hambartsi Tone 4 [DK - D-flat / Fourth Side]: "Glory to Your resurrection, O Lord" (P'ark' harut'yan k'o ter); "Having taken upon Yourself the cross" (Khach'il i yandzn) to the end.

19. Monday. Tone 1 [AD - A / First Class]. [Feast of] Anton the Anchorite.

20. Tuesday. Tone 1 [AK - A-flat / First Side]. [Feast of] Theodosius the King and the Holy Babes of Ephesus.

21. Wednesday. Tone 2 [BD - B / Second Class]. Penitential Father [Harts] Tone 1 [AK - A-flat / First Side]: "Glorified forever is the God of our fathers" (P'aravoryal havytyan astvats harts'n merots').

22. Thursday. Tone 2 [BK - B-flat / Second Side]. [Feast of] Cyriacus and his mother Julitta, and of the witnesses Gordius, Polyeuctus, and Gregory. Father [Harts] Tone 1 [A-dz - A / First Class]: "You, the righteous and upright judge" (Uzk'ez ardar yev ughigh da...).

24. Saturday. Tone 3 [GK - G-flat / Third Side]. [Feast of] Patriarchs Athanasius and Cyril, and Gregory the Theologian.

25. Sunday. Tone 4 [DD - D / Fourth Class]. Second [Sunday]. Father [Harts] Tone 2 [BK - B-flat / Second Side]: "By Your voluntary will You came to suffering" (Kamavorut'yamb yekir i ch'arch'arans). Hambartsi Tone 4 [DD - D / Fourth Class]: "The archangel [announced] the resurrection of the Son of God" (Zyarut'yun astvatsordvoyn hreshtakapetn).

26. Monday. Tone 4 [DK - D-flat / Fourth Side]. [Feast of] Vahan of Goghtn, and Eugenia the Virgin, and her father Philip, and her mother Claudia, and the two brothers Sergius and Apiton, and the two eunuchs Protus and Hyacinthus. Father [Harts] Tone 1 [AK - A-flat / First Side]: "Holy God, Creator of eternity" (Surb astvats ararich' yavityenits').

27. Tuesday. Tone 1 [AD - A / First Class]. [Feast of] Tryphon, Barsamas, and Onuphrius the Anchorites.

28. Wednesday. Tone 1 [AK - A-flat / First Side]. Penitential Father [Harts] Tone 2 [B-dz - B / Second Class]: "Who saved the three youths from the furnace" (Vor abrets'uts'er zeris mankunsn i hnoc'inn).

29. Thursday. Tone 2 [BD - B / Second Class]. [Feast of] Eugenius, Macarius, Valerius, Candidus, and Aquilas. Father [Harts] Tone 1 [AK - A-flat / First Side]: "Who are exalted and rest in the saints" (Vor bardzryald es yev i surbs hanguts'yal).

31. Saturday. Tone 3 [GD - G / Third Class]. [Feast of] Thaddeus the Apostle and Sandukht the Virgin. For the Apostle Thaddeus, start from the order set down below in the First Volume after Vardavar; but the Father [Harts] hymns and the tones are to be taken here.

In February:

1. Sunday. Tone 3 [GK - G-flat / Third Side]. Third [Sunday]. Father [Harts] Tone 3 [GD - G / Third Class]: "Creator of all creation, O Christ, today" (Ararich'd araradsots' K'ristos aysor). Hambartsi Tone 3 [GK - G-flat / Third Side]: "The sight of the lightning-like angel on the stone" (Tesil p'aylaknadzev zvart'noyn i vimin).

2. Monday. Tone 4 [DD - D / Fourth Class]. [Feast of] Cyprian the Bishop and the forty-five martyrs, and of the virgins Justina, Euphemia, and Christina. Father [Harts] Tone 2 [BD - B / Second Class]: "You who do not harbor resentment and are most merciful, do not scatter us" (Anokhakal yev baregut' mi ts'rer).

3. Tuesday. Tone 4 [DK - D-flat / Fourth Side]. [Feast of] Athenogenes the Bishop, and his ten disciples, and the five martyrs. Father [Harts] Tone 2 [BD - B / Second Class]: "With Your saints we praise You" (Und surbs k'o barebanemk' uzk'ez).
