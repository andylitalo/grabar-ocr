## English

CANON
OF THE GLORIOUS TRANSFIGURATION OF CHRIST OUR GOD
ON MOUNT TABOR
WHICH IS VARDAVAR SUNDAY.

In the Night [Service] there is a vigil. They shall place the Holy Sacrament upon the cushion-throne in the chancel. Processional Hymn, Tone 8 [dzk]: "This day in glory rejoices" (Tsndzay aysor parok). And the priests shall vest together and come out to the chancel, having lighted candles in their hands, and they shall begin the Blessing Hymn, Tone 1 [adz]: "He who was transfigured on the mountain" (Vor i lerinn aylakerpyal). Alleluia. "Yours are the heavens." Verse: "The north and the south." Alternate [verse]: "The heights of the mountains." "Come, let us worship." Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 2 [ak]: "The ineffable" (Zanchareli), with its proper canon. And they shall go out in procession with the Cross and the Gospel and all adornments, and they shall say the "Lord of Heaven" [hymns]. Litany: "Let us say." [Trisagion]: "Holy God, who wast revealed on Mount Tabor." And then Hymn, Tone 1 [adz]: "He who was transfigured on the mountain." Afterwards, Psalm 97 [98]: "The mountains shall rejoice." Verse: "Bless." Proverbs of Solomon [Ecclesiastes] 11:9: "Rejoice, O young man" to chapter 12:7: "who gave it." Prophet Isaiah 34:20: "Behold Zion" to verse 22: "He will save us." Catholic [Epistle], 2 Peter 1:12: "Wherefore I intend" to verse 19: "in your hearts." Alleluia: "The heights of the mountains." Gospel according to Mark 8:27: "And Jesus went out" to chapter 9:12: "of him." And they shall bless the four corners of the world [Andastan]. Litany: "By this Holy Cross." Prayer: "Guardian." And upon returning, Hymn, Tone 8 [dk], melody: "The mountains shall rejoice." And then "Newly-created" (Norasteghtsyal). Litany: "Let us ask," "Save us." Prayer: "To your almighty and wondrous Transfiguration."

At the Midday Liturgy, Introit: "Your Lordship is revealed today." Verse: "My soul shall magnify." Hymn, Tone 3 [bz]: "Rejoice, O crowned." Psalm 14 [15]: "Lord, who shall abide." Proverbs of Wisdom 7:25: "For she is the breath" to chapter 8:4: "of his works." Prophet Zechariah 14:16: "And it shall come to pass, that everyone" to verse 21: "in that day." Catholic [Epistle], 1 John 1:1: "That which was from the beginning" to verse 7: "from all sin." Alleluia: "A bright cloud." Gospel according to Matthew 16:13: "When Jesus came" to chapter 17:13: "he spoke unto them." Eucharistic Hymn [Srbatsutyun]: "Multitudes."

In the Evening at the tenth hour they shall assemble in the church, and they shall begin in order the morning psalm and read the scriptures, as written above. And at the conclusion, Orthi [Stand up]. Gospel according to Matthew 17:14: "And when they were come" to verse 20: "and fasting." And then they shall go out in procession with the Cross and the Gospel. Hymn, Tone 5 [gdz]: "On Mount Tabor" (I tabor i lerinn). Afterwards, Orthi [Stand up]. Gospel according to Mark 8:39 [9:1]: "And he said unto them" to chapter 9:12: "of him." Hymn, Tone 8 [dzk]: "Today the mystery" (Aysor zkhorhurt). Afterwards, Orthi [Stand up]. Gospel according to John 7:1: "After these things Jesus walked" to verse 13: "for fear of the Jews." And they shall bless the four corners of the world [Andastan]. Litany: "By this Holy Cross."
