Midday Entry-Hymn: "We confess you, O Mother of God" [Asduadzadzin zkez].
Psalm 109 [110], Antiphon: "In holy splendor." Epistle: Galatians 4:1, "Now I say... to verse 7... and an heir through God." Alleluia: "Rejoice, O highly favored." Gospel: Luke 1:26, "Now in the sixth month... to verse 38... and the angel departed from her."

On the Fifth Day of the Theophany: Morning Tone 3 [ahg - gyuz]: "Today He who is on the throne" [Aysor vor yator], with its canon. Gospel: John 1:18, "No one has seen God at any time; the only begotten... to verse 28... was baptizing, and he was there."
Midday Entry-Hymn: "We beseech you" [Aghachemkk zkez]. Psalm 98 [99], Antiphon: "Exalt the Lord." Response: "The Lord reigns, let the peoples tremble." Epistle: Hebrews 12:17 [12:18], "For you have not come... to verse 27... so that those things which cannot be shaken may remain." Alleluia: "And you, child, will be called the prophet." Gospel: Luke 1:39, "Now Mary arose... to verse 56... and returned to her house."

On the Sixth Day of the Theophany: Morning Tone 2 [ahf - pyur]: "Without beginning" [Anasgbanagan], with its canon. Gospel: John 1:29, "The next day John saw... to verse 34... This is the Son of God."
Midday Entry-Hymn: "O Mother of God, unwedded" [Asduadzadzin anharsnatsyal]. Psalm 29 [30], Antiphon: "O Lord, You brought me up." Response: "I will extol You." Epistle: 1 Thessalonians 4:12 [4:13], "But I do not want you to be ignorant, brethren... to verse 18... with these words." Alleluia: "He brought me out." Gospel: John 11:1, "Now a certain man was sick... to verse 46... and told them what He had done."

On the Seventh Day of the Theophany: Morning Tone 3 [ahg - pyur]: "Incomprehensible" [Aneghaneli], with its canon. Gospel: John 1:35, "Again, the next day, John stood... to verse 51... upon the Son of Man."
Midday Entry-Hymn: "We confess you, O Mother of God" [Asduadzadzin zkez khostovanyal]. Psalm 97 [98], Antiphon: "Proclaim the good news." Response: "Sing to the Lord." Epistle: Romans 1:1, "Paul, a bondservant of Jesus Christ... to verse 7... and from the Lord Jesus Christ." Alleluia: "The Lord has sworn." Gospel: Luke 2:1, "And it came to pass in those days... to verse 7... room in the inn."

The Eighth Day is the Feast of the Birth and Circumcision of our Lord Jesus Christ. Morning Tone 4 [ahf - dyu]: "Light from Light" [Loys i luso], with its canon. After "The Lord from heaven" [Ter hergnitsn], without psalm, is said: "The Son." Gospel: Luke 2:8, "Now there were shepherds..." up to the point where it says: "Glory to God in the highest."
As on the night of the Nativity, they shall say the first verse of the "Glory in the highest," and then read the remainder of the Gospel: Luke 2:15, "And it came to pass, when they had gone... to verse 20... as it had been told them." And they shall say the other verse: "And at all times." Morning Song: "Sun of Righteousness" [Aregagn artarutyan]. Psalm 109 [110], Antiphon: "In holy splendor." Response: "The Lord said to my Lord." Gospel: Luke 3:1, "Now in the fifteenth year... to Luke 4:13... for a season."
Midday Entry-Hymn: "We beseech you" [Aghachemkk zkez]. Psalm 97 [98], Antiphon: "The Lord showed." Response: "Bless the Lord." Epistle: Colossians 2:8, "Beware lest... to verse 15... by His resurrection." Alleluia: "Rejoice, O highly favored." Gospel: Luke 2:21, "And when eight days were completed... to verse 21... in the womb."
But in the evening, the [Hambartsi] Tone 3 [ahg]: "Light from Light" [Loys i luso], say the three together.
Afterwards, Litany: "Holy Mother of God" [Surp zasduadzadzin]. Prayer: "Receive, Lord, our supplications" [Yngal der zagachans].

From here until the Eve of the Fast of Catechumens [Arachavori], always consult the Calendar of the year to find the duration of the intervening period, and concerning the upcoming feasts, how and where they ought to be celebrated. Likewise for the Sundays, Wednesdays, and Fridays of these weeks, together with their appropriate [Hargh] hymns, the Year-Letter will indicate them to you; you shall see the pattern here.
