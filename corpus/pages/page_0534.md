## English

Wednesday. Feast of the Church. Tone 3 [g3]: "Rejoice, O Holy Church" (Urakh ler surb yegeghetsi).
Canticle [Harts] Tone 3: "Today resplendent" (Aysor paytsar), with its canon [sarokn].
Morning Song: "Holiness is fitting to Your house" (Tan kum vayel e). Litany: "Let us say" (Asasguk).
Patronymic [Mankunk]: Of the Cross, from the tone of the day. Then: "By the Holy Church" (Surb yegeghetsyavs), "Save [us]" (Ketso). Prayer: "At the door of this holy temple" (Ar dran surb tajaris).

Midday Liturgy
Introit: "For peace" (Zkhaghaghutyun).
Response [Phokh]: "The Lord reigns" (Ter thag).
Hymn Tone 3 [g3]: "Today rejoicing" (Aysor urakhatsyal).
Psalm 147: "Praise Jerusalem."
Prophet Zephaniah 3:14 "Rejoice greatly" to verse 18 "your afflicted".
Epistle: Hebrews 9:11 "But Christ being come" to verse 15 "eternal".
Alleluia: "The Lord is in His holy temple."
Gospel: Matthew 16:13 "And when Jesus came" to verse 12 [actually 16:19/20 or similar, reflecting "loosed in heaven"].

In the Evening
Messedi: "Bless the Lord here" (Ast orhnetsek), "Who stand" (O kayq).
Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 2 [bk]: "Ineffable" (Anpatumn). Litany: "By the Holy Church" (Surb yegeghetsyavs), "Save [us]" (Ketso). Prayer: "In the midst of this temple" (I mej tajaris).

Thursday. Feast of the Church. Tone 2 [bk]: "Ineffable" (Anpatumn) and "He who is co-essential" (Or hamagoyd), which is of the Resurrection; find and recite it. Canticle [Harts]: Say that of the Ark, Tone 3 [g3]: "Today they rejoice" (Aysor tsndan), with its canon [sarokn].
Song: "Holiness is fitting to Your house" (Tan kum vayel e). Litany: "Let us say" (Asasguk).
Patronymic [Mankunk]: Of the Cross, from the tone of the day. Then: "By the Holy Church" (Surb yegeghetsyavs), "Save [us]" (Ketso). Prayer: "At the door of this holy temple" (Ar dran surb tajaris).

Midday Liturgy

Alleluia: "How beloved are."
Gospel: Matthew 21:12 "And Jesus entered" to verse 17 "and lodged there".

In the Evening

Song: "You have given a banner" (Etur yerkyugha). Litany: "Let us say" (Asasguk).
Patronymic [Mankunk]: Of the Cross, from the tone of the day. Then: "By Your Holy Cross" (Surb khachivs), "Save [us]" (Ketso). Prayer: "Protect [us]" (Pahpanea).
