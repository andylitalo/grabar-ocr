Ezekiel 4:16: "And the word of the Lord came... to verse 19: ...you shall save [your soul]."
The Apostle: 2 Corinthians 6:1: "We as workers together with you... to verse 14: ...yoked with unbelievers."
Alleluia: "Your priests [shall put on righteousness]."
Gospel: Matthew 12:22: "Then was brought... to verse 32: ...in the world to come."
And in the evening, Hambartsi [Hymn of Ascent] Tone 4 [dch]: "The Word Who with the Father."
And drawing the curtain, they shall cover the altars, in the likeness of the expulsion of Adam from paradise, and they shall not kiss the Gospel henceforth; neither shall they raise [it] during the "We believe" [Creed] and during "Save, Lord" [Ketso] for the congregation to see; also, they shall say the "Joyful Light" [Phos Hilaron] by number [recited without chant] in place of the supplication until Palm Sunday.
--[Separator]--
On the Sunday of the True Eve of Great Lent [Bun Barekendan]:
Change the tone at the end to Tone 6 [gk]. But the first canon is "Confess [the Lord]" and the last canon is "In my distress."
Canon Leader: "Bless the Lord, O my soul; I will praise the Lord in my life, I will sing psalm to my God as long as I exist."
Say the exchange [verse] in full number, and at the end in chant:
"I will bless You with thanksgiving in my life, I will sing psalm to You, Christ the King, with a new victorious song of praise." Repeat thrice.
Then by number: "Praise the Lord, for a psalm is good, let praise be sweet to our God." Say the exchange in full, then in chant:
"May the thanksgivings of those who fear Your name be sweet to You, O Christ, which You have made them hope for by Your mercy in the mysteries of Your life-giving coming." Repeat thrice.
Again by number: "Praise the Lord, O Jerusalem." Say the exchange in full, then in chant:
"Your holy redeemed church praises You, O Christ; rejoicing, it sees You, the Savior, coming in Fatherly authority to crown Your saints." Repeat thrice.
Then: "Today ineffable [the hymn]."
Psalm: "A sign was shown [Psalm 4:6]."
Gospel of the Crucifixion; and one verse of the song from "The Renewer" [Norogogh], and then "For the Souls" [Hogvots]; for during Great Lent, there is no Rest Hymn [Hangstean Sharakan] nor "God ineffable."
Morning Hymn Tone 8 [dk]: "The Word Who with the Father," with its canon.
Myrrh-bearing [oil-bearing] Gospel: Matthew 27:57: "And when it was evening... to verse 61: ...opposite the tomb."
On the Sundays of Great Lent, read only [the passage of] the Burial from the Myrrh-bearing Gospel. After this, say three times:
"All the days of my life, I will not cease blessing You, Christ, Savior of the world," but the last time aloud. Say this until Easter Sunday.
And then say "In the night" Tone 8 [de].
Morning Gospel: Matthew 6:22: "The lamp of the body is the eye... to [7:27]: ...and great was its fall."
And say only the first two stanzas of "Newly Created" [Norasteghdzeal]. Afterward: "Save, Lord" [Ketso], without the Gospel. This is the order of the Sundays of Lent.
At Midday [Liturgy]:
Psalm 64 [65]: "Praise is due to You."
Prophet: Isaiah 58:1: "Cry aloud with power... to verse 14: ...[the mouth of the Lord] has spoken this."
Psalm 75 [76]: "Make vows." Verse: "In Judah is [God] known."
The Apostle: Romans 13:11: "And know this... to 14:25 [16:27]: ...glory forever, Amen."
Alleluia Psalm 99 [100]: "Shout joyfully to the Lord," say in full.
Gospel: Matthew 6:1: "Take heed to your almsgiving... to verse 21: ...there your heart will be also."
On these Sundays of Lent, at the Midday Liturgy, say the first Blessing [Orhnutiun] hymn, and the last one at the Evening Hambartsi [Hymn of Ascent]; and at the Divine Liturgy, the Rest Hymn, "Profound Mystery" [Khorhurd Khorin], and "In This Sanctuary of Offerings" do not take place.
Moreover, from here until the first Sunday of the Coming of the Holy Spirit [Pentecost], all the established items—namely feasts, Patristic [Harts] hymns, intermediate periods, and tones—remain always fixed, except for the Presentation of the Lord [Tiarn'ndaraj] and the Annunciation, and the daily tones are also indicated by the daily Blessing [Orhnutiun] hymns, as you see.
--[Separator]--
On the first Monday of the Fast:
Hymn of Repentance [Harts], say in order, Tone 1 [ad]: "Who lookest with sweetness," but do not say the "At-the-Dawn" [Aghotsm] hymns.
Midday Mesedi: Psalm 50 [51]: "Turn [Your face] from me, Lord." Verse: "Have mercy on me, O God."
Prophet: Isaiah 1:16: "Wash yourselves, make yourselves clean... to verse 20: ...[the mouth of the Lord] has spoken this."
On these two days at Midday, say only the "Have Mercy" [Voghormia] hymns, as is customary.
