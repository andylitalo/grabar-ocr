## English

If on other days: [sing] the Song of Creation here, and after it, "Hidden Mystery" (Dzadzkeal khorhourtn).
At the Midday Liturgy, Entrance Hymn (Zhamamout): "Mother of God, Unwedded" (Astuadzatsin anhars). Anthem (Pokh): "My soul magnifies" (Metsatsustse). Hymn Tone 6 [gki]: "Tabernacle of the Mother of God" (Astuadzatsin khoran).
The Psalm, Scripture Reading, and Gospel are those of the morning. Alleluia: "Rejoice, O favored one" (Urakh ler berkreal). Hymn of the Censing (Srbasatsutiun): "Multitude" (Bazmutiun).
But if it falls on Palm Sunday, or on Holy Thursday, or on Holy Friday, or on Holy Saturday, or on Easter Sunday: before the Blessing (Orhnutiun), extract the hymn "Ineffable Mystery" (Khorhourdn anchar) with its auxiliary hymns (sar), and then read the Scripture and the Gospel. Afterward, the Litany: "By the Holy Mother of God" (Surb zAstuadzatsinn). Prayer: "Receive, Lord" (Inkal ter). And then begin the proper service of the day.
And if it falls on the Saturday of Lazarus, or on Holy Monday, Holy Tuesday, or Holy Wednesday: before the Blessing, in place of the service hymn, extract the Blessing of the day along with its auxiliary hymns (sar). Read the Scripture of the day and the Gospel, and say the Song of Nerses and "For the Souls" (Hogvotsn). And then begin to perform the Feast of the Annunciation, saying the Blessing "Ineffable Mystery" (Khorhourdn anchar) according to the order, and the rest.
Do likewise if it falls during Eastertide (Yinank). But read the Gospel of the day after the Scripture and Gospel of the Annunciation; then "Save, O Lord" (Ketso).
Likewise, if it falls on Low Sunday (Ashkharhamatran Kiurake): after the Gospel of the Annunciation, read the Scripture and Gospel of the Antasdan [Blessing of the Fields], and perform the Antasdan. Then [read] the Gospel of the day, and "Newly Created" (Norasteghtseal) and "Save, O Lord" (Ketso).
But at the Midday Liturgy and in the Evening: the Psalms, Scriptures, and Gospels are of the Resurrection, etc. As for the Evening Hymn [Hambartsi]: on Saturday evening [sing] the Hymn of the Coming here, and on other days, that of the Annunciation, along with "Holy Mother" (Mayr surb).

**Second Sunday [GD], which is also called New Sunday (Krkynazatik) [Low Sunday].**
In the night, there is a Vigil. Canon head: "Bless the Lord, O my soul" (Orhneandzn im). Anthem (Pokh): "I will praise You with thanksgiving" (Orhnetsits zkez gohutyamp), as on the night of Great Barekendan. Night Hymn Tone 6 [gki]: "Renewed Islands" (Norogeal kghzik), with its auxiliary hymns (sar). Say the great Blessing after it.
Morning Psalm 84 [85]: "Show us, O Lord." Anthem (Pokh): "Lord, Thou hast been favorable unto Thy land." Until the Ascension, this is the morning Psalm.
Gospel: Luke 4:14 to verse 30: "he passed through the midst of them."
Afterward: "Newly Created" (Norasteghtseal). Litany: "Let us ask" (Khndresgouk). "Save, O Lord" (Ketso). Prayer: "To Your Almighty."
At the Midday Liturgy, Entrance Hymn (Zhamamout): "Christ is risen" (Kristos yareav). Until the Ascension, on every Sunday and Saturday, say "Christ is risen." But on other days, say "Awesome" (Ahavor) and "Since He rose" (Kanzi yareav).
Psalm 22 [23]: "The Lord is my shepherd." "The earth is the Lord's" [Psalm 23/24]. "Unto Thee, O Lord, do I lift up" [Psalm 24/25].
Acts 5:31 [or 5:17/34] to 6:7: "were obedient to the faith."
Psalm 64 [65]: "Praise waiteth for Thee."
Catholic Epistle: James 3:1 to verse 12: "make sweet water."
Alleluia Psalm 147: "Praise the Lord, O Jerusalem."
Gospel: John 1:1 to verse 17: "came by [Jesus] Christ."
In the Evening, First Psalm 149: "Sing unto the Lord a new song." Until the Ascension, this is the first Psalm.
Gospel: John 21:15 to verse 25: "that should be written."
And then they shall go out with the Cross and Gospel for the Antasdan [Blessing of the Fields].
Hymn Tone 8 [dki]: "Today the Victorious Sign" (Aysor nshan haghtogh).
Afterward, Psalm 4: "Thou hast set a sign." Anthem (Pokh): "When I call."
Gospel: Matthew 27:50 to verse 61: "over against the sepulchre."
And they shall sign the fields with the cross.
Litany: "By this Holy Cross" (Surb khachivs). Prayer: "Guardian" (Pahpanich).
And upon returning, they shall begin "Bow down [Thine ear]" (Khonarhetso) and "Deliver [me]" (Apretso).
Messedi: "The God of gods." Anthem (Pokh): "From the rising of the sun."

Psalm 150: "Praise God in His sanctuary." Until the Ascension, this is the dismissal Psalm.
Gospel: John 20:26 to verse 31: "through His name."
