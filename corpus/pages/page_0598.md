7. Monday. Tone 1 [Adz]. Fast of the Holy Mother of God. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 3 [Bz]: "Blessed art thou, Lord God of our fathers" (Օրհնեալ տէր աստուած հարցն մերոց).

13. Sunday. Tone 7 [Dz]. Assumption of the Holy Mother of God. The eating period [non-fasting season] is four weeks. See in order in the First Volume, keeping the proper order of this place.

23. Tuesday. Tone 1 [Adz]. Fast day. Canticle of the Fathers of the Fast, Tone 4 [Bk]: "Blessed art thou of our fathers" (ԶՀարցըն մերոց օրհնեալ).

26. Saturday. Tone 4 [Bk]. Feast of Thomas the Apostle. See also the others in order in the First Volume.

September

2. Saturday. Tone 3 [Bz]. Feast of James and Simon the Apostles. Read the scripture and the gospel of the Tuesday of the Fast of Saint James.

3. Sunday. Tone 4 [Bk]. Fourth Sunday. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 5 [Gz]: "We glorify thee" (Փառաւորեմք զքեզ). Hymn of the Dwelling, Tone 4 [Bk]: "Christ is risen" (Քրիստոս յարեաւ).

4. Monday. Tone 5 [Gz]. Feast of Andrew the General and his army, etc.

5. Tuesday. Tone 6 [Gk]. Feast of Adrian and his wife Anatolia, etc. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 6 [Gk]: "To thee, O God" (Առ քեզ աստուած).

7. Thursday. Tone 8 [Dk]. Feast of Abraham and Khoren, Cosmas and Damian, and Astuadzatur the Martyr. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 8 [Dk]: "Who with wondrous glory" (Որ զհրաշափառ).

8. Friday. Tone 1 [Adz]. Feast of the Nativity of the Holy Mother of God.

9. Saturday. Tone 2 [Ak]. Feast of the Holy Council of Nicaea, of the three hundred and eighteen patriarchs.

10. Sunday. Tone 3 [Bz]. Fifth Sunday. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 6 [Gk]: "Ineffable" (Անճառելի). Hymn of the Dwelling, Tone 3 [Bz]: "At the tomb" (Ի գերեզման) and "They came to the tomb" (Եկին ի գերեզ). Barekendan [Eve] of the Fast of the Holy Cross.

11. Monday. Tone 4 [Bk]. Fast of the Holy Cross. Canticle of the Fathers of the Fast, Tone 4 [Bk]: "Lord God of our fathers" (Տէր աստուած հարցն մերոց).

17. Sunday. Tone 2 [Ak]. Exaltation of the Holy Cross. See until the tenth Sunday in the First Volume; but for the canticle of the martyrs and of the fasts, and the resurrection hymns of the dwelling, and the tone of the days, see under the letter 'G' (Ղ) of the Directory, keeping this month.

November

19. Sunday. Tone 1 [Adz]. Tenth Sunday. Hymn of the Dwelling, Tone 1 [Adz]: "Who by ineffable essence" (Որ անճառ էութեամբ). Barekendan [Eve] of the Fifty-day Fast [Yisnakats].

20. Monday. Tone 2 [Ak]. Fast of the Fifty-day Fast. Canticle of the Fathers of the Fast, Tone 5 [Gz]: "Blessed art thou forever" (Օրհնեալ ես յաւիտեան).

21. Tuesday. Tone 3 [Bz]. Fast day. Feast of the Presentation of the Holy Mother of God.

25. Saturday. Tone 7 [Dz]. Feast of Gregory and Nicholas the Wonderworking Patriarchs, and the other Nicholas the Patriarch, and Myron the Bishop. Magnificat (Մանկունք), on the proper tone: "They were amazed" (Հիացան). Patarag [Liturgy] Hymn, Tone 3: "Who by light" (Որ լուսով).

26. Sunday. Tone 8 [Dk]. First Sunday. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 2 [Ak]: "Unoriginate Word" (Անսկիղբն բանդ). Hymn of the Dwelling, Tone 8 [Dk]: "The hosts of angels" (Հրեշտակացն դասք) and "Thy angel, O Lord" (Հրեշտակ քո տէր).

27. Monday. Tone 1 [Adz]. Feast of Julian and Vasilouhi. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 4 [Bk]: "We bless thee, Lord" (Օրհնեմք զքեզ տէր). See also the others in the First Volume, keeping the proper order of this place.
