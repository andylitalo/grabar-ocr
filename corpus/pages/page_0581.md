12. Sunday. Tone 6 [GK]. Assumption of the Holy Mother of God. The track of the meat-eating period is four weeks. See the First Volume up to the third Sunday.

September:
2. Sunday. Tone 2 [BZ]. Fourth. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 7 [G3]: "We glorify You" (Paravoremk zk'ez). Hymn of the Evening (Hambartsi), Tone 3 [BDZ]: "At the Tomb" (I gerezman), and "They came to the tomb" (Yekin i gerez).
3. Monday. Tone 4 [BK]. Andrew the General, and his army, etc.
4. Tuesday. Tone 5 [GCH]. Adrian and his wife Natalia, etc. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 2 [AK]: "Holy God Creator" (Surb astuats ararich).
6. Thursday. Tone 7 [DCH]. Abraham and Khoren, Cosmas and Damian, and Astuatsatur the Martyr. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 4 [K]: "You who are exalted" (Vor bardzreald es).
8. Saturday. Tone 1 [ADZCH]. Feast of the Nativity of the Holy Mother of God.
9. Sunday. Tone 2 [AK]. Fifth. Hymn of the Evening (Hambartsi), Tone 2 [AK]: "Resurrected" (Yaruts'eal). Barekendan [Eve] of the Fast of the Holy Cross.
10. Monday. Tone 3 [BZCHZ]. Fast of the Holy Cross. Canticle of the Fathers (Lamentations), Tone 4 [BK]: "The voice of the youths" (Mankantsn zdzayn).
16. Sunday. Tone 1 [AZ]. Exaltation of the Holy Cross. See up to the tenth Sunday in the First Volume, keeping the order of this section.
23. Sunday. Tone 8 [DK]. Second [Sunday after Cross]. Hymn of the Evening (Hambartsi), Tone 8 [DK]: "The choirs of angels" (Hreshtakatsn dask). Barekendan [Eve] of the Holy Cross of Varag.
24. Monday. Tone 1 [ADZZ]. Fast. Mamas, etc. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 3 [B3]: "Without malice" (Anokhakal).
26. Wednesday. Tone 2 [BZ]. Fast. Canticle of the Fathers (Lamentations), Tone 8 [DZ]: "God of our Fathers" (Harts'n merots astuats).
30. Sunday. Tone 7 [DZ]. Third. Feast of the Holy Cross of Varag. The meat-eating period is seven weeks.

October:
7. Sunday. Tone 6 [GK]. Fourth. Hymn of the Evening (Hambartsi), Tone 6 [GK]: "Divine" (Zastuatsayin), and "Your Resurrection" (Ezko yarut'iund).
14. Sunday. Tone 5 [GZ]. Fifth. Hymn of the Evening (Hambartsi), Tone 7 [G3]: "Myrrh-bearing women" (Yughaber), and "Today the Resurrection" (Aysor yarut'iun), and "With the sign of the Cross" (I khachanich).
21. Sunday. Tone 4 [BK]. Sixth. Hymn of the Evening (Hambartsi), Tone 4 [BK]: "Christ is risen" (K'ristos yareav), and "Morning" (Aravotean).
Sunday. Tone 3 [BDZ]. Seventh. Discovery of the Cross.
Tuesday. Tone 5 [GZ]. Hyperichius, and Severian of Sebastea, etc. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 2 [AK]: "You who are exalted" (Vor bardzreald es).
Wednesday. Tone 6 [GK]. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 7 [DZ]: "God of our Fathers who saved" (Harts'n merots astuats vor abretsuts'er). And see the others according to the order.

November:
Saturday. Tone 1 [AZ]. All Saints: ancient and new, etc.
Sunday. Tone 2 [AK]. Eighth. Hymn of the Evening (Hambartsi), Tone 2 [AK]: "Wonderful" (Sk'ancheli).
Sunday. Tone 1 [AZ]. Ninth. Hymn of the Evening (Hambartsi), Tone 1 [AZ]: "With the women" (End kanaysn), to the end.
Sunday. Tone 8 [DK]. Tenth. Hymn of the Evening (Hambartsi), Tone 8 [DGK]: "Your angel" (Hreshtak k'o), to the end. Barekendan [Eve] of the Fifty-Day Fast [Advent].
Monday. Tone 1 [AZ]. Fast of Advent. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 7 [DZ]: "You who showed in all things" (Vor yamenayni tsuts'er).
Wednesday. Tone 2 [BZ]. Fast. Feast of the Presentation of the Holy Mother of God.
Sunday. Tone 7 [DZ]. First [Sunday of Advent]. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 3 [B3]: "You who are King" (Vor t'agavord es). Hymn of the Evening (Hambartsi), Tone 5 [G3]: "Awesome" (Ahagin), and "You who are above" (Vor i verd es).
Monday. Tone 8 [DK]. Julian and Vasilissa. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 8 [D3]: "Those who trust" (Yusats'ealk'n).
And see the others in the First Volume.

December:
Sunday. Tone 6 [GK]. Second. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 4 [BK]: "Who [took the form] of a servant" (Vor zdzarayi). Hymn of the Evening (Hambartsi), Tone 6 [GK]: "Assuming our [nature]" (Areal ev mer).
Monday. Tone 7 [DZCH]. Gennarius and Mercurius, and James and Phocas.
Tuesday. Tone 8 [DK]. Abgar the King.
Sunday. Tone 5 [GZ]. Third. Feast of the Conception of the Holy Virgin. Barekendan [Eve] of the Fast of Saint James.
Wednesday. Tone 8 [DK]. Canticle of the Fathers (Lamentations), Tone 8 [DK]: "Blessed art thou, Lord God of our Fathers" (Orhneal ter astuats harts'n merots). "Almighty" (Amenazor).
Sunday. Tone 4 [BK]. Fourth. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 3 [E3]: "Glorified Son of God" (Paravoreal vordi astutso). Hymn of the Evening (Hambartsi), Tone 4 [BK]: "The women anticipated" (Kankhets'in kanayk'n).
