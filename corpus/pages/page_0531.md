## English

THE RULE [CANON]
OF THE EXALTATION OF THE HOLY CROSS

On the Sunday of the Exaltation of the Holy Cross. In the night there is a vigil; they shall place the Holy Relics upon the cushions in the chancel. Processional hymn, Tone 7 [dzy / dzk]: "The Power of the Holy Cross" (Zorutyun surb khachi). And the priests shall vest together and come into the chancel, and they shall begin the morning hymn, Tone 1 [ah / ay]: "He who [stretched out] your spotless arms" (Or zanarat bazuks ko), with its canons. 
Morning song: "You have given a sign to those who fear you."
Litany: "Let us say."
"Holy God," [who was] crucified [for us]. 
And they shall go forth with the Cross, the Gospel, and all adornments in procession, saying the hymn Tone 1 [ay]: "He who [stretched out] your spotless arms" (Or zanarat bazuks).
Psalm 4: "There was signed" [Show us the light of your countenance]; Response: "When I called."
Proverbs 14:1 "Again, if [one preparing to] sail..." to verse 8: "...the one who made it."
Prophet Ezekiel 9:2 "And behold, six men..." to verse 6: "...do not come near."
Apostle 1 Corinthians 1:18 "For the preaching of the cross..." to verse 24: "...[the power of God and the] wisdom [of God]."
Alleluia: "There was signed."
Gospel Matthew 24:27 "For as the lightning..." to verse 35: "...shall not pass away."
Litany: "For the peace from above."
And they shall bless the four corners of the world [Andastan].
Afterwards, Litany: "By your Holy Cross."
Prayer: "Protect us."
And then: "Newly created" (Norasteghtsyal).
Litany: "Let us beseech," "Save [us]."
Prayer: "To your almighty."
And then they shall place the Holy Cross and the Gospel upon the stand, and the priests and the congregation shall bow down together, saying the hymn Tone 2 [bk]: "Before your Cross, O Christ, we bow down, and your holy burial we magnify, and your holy resurrection we glorify," etc.
Likewise: "Glory to your holy Cross, Alleluia; to your crucifixion, Alleluia; to your holy resurrection, Alleluia," etc., as at Easter.
Afterwards, Litany: "By your Holy Cross."
Prayer: "Protect us."

At the Midday Liturgy, Introit: "Before the honorable."
Response: "The Lord reigns."
Hymn Tone 6 [gy / gzk]: "Victorious" and "A new blessing."
Psalm 4: "There was signed."
Prophet Isaiah 49:13 "Be glad [O heavens]..." to verse 23: "...they waited for me."
Apostle Galatians 6:14 "But God forbid that I [should glory]..." to verse 18: "...brethren, Amen."
Alleluia: "Give power."
Gospel John 3:13 "And no man hath ascended..." to verse 21: "...they are wrought [in God]."
Hymn of the Hagiology: "Who is like [unto our God]."

But before the evening hour, they shall place the Holy Cross, which contains a portion of the True Wood, on a silver platter in the vestry; and they shall adorn it with basil and rosewater, and they shall cover it with a precious cloth.

And at the ninth hour, they shall assemble in the church, and the Bishop shall vest along with the priests and deacons, and they shall go with censers, lanterns, and liturgical fans to the Holy Cross. And two senior priests shall lift up the Holy Cross on the platter.
