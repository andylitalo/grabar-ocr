## English

A BRIEF PREAMBLE
CONCERNING THE SECOND VOLUME OF THIS TONATSUYTS [DIRECTORY]
... [untranslatable string of index letters]

First, let it be known that:
This Second Volume was created as a rule and guide for the First Volume.
Because those intervals of time which increase and decrease, and whose specific details the First Volume cannot display,
as well as the feasts that fall within such intervals and, being movable, cannot be celebrated in the same place [every year]—
this Second Volume displays these, regulated in all details, as you can see.
But those intervals and feasts which are immovable, and which are set down in the First Volume, always remain unchanged in the same way;
this Second Volume does not mention or display them, but instructs you to look in the First Volume in each respective place, as you can see.
Yet, within these intervals, certain feasts that are sometimes relocated due to the requirement of the Calendar Letter, and do not remain the same,
this Second Volume lists by name and mentions.
Therefore, do not be confused by such things; for whatever this Second Volume mentions,
know that these things are altered in that year.
It also mentions the [hymns of the] Fathers [Harts], the days of the month, and the daily tones, so that you may know them.
And whatever it leaves out and does not mention, you must look up in the First Volume and proceed according to the order.
For this reason, it is written in many places in this Second Volume: "See Volume 1," preserving the order of the place,
so that you may know that you must always keep the table of the Calendar Letter in hand alongside this Second Volume,
and proceed according to its indications with the First Volume.

Second: This Second Volume instructs you to look for certain intervals of some calendar letters under other letters,
as you can see under B, C, E, and so on.
The reason for this is that if we had put everything under every letter, there would have been much repetition, and the Tonatsuyts book would have grown too large.
Therefore, just as we directed you to look in the First Volume for the immovable feasts,
likewise, concerning certain movable intervals and feasts,
if they were found to be identical in form under some letters,
we placed them in full under one calendar letter in this Second Volume,
and under the other matching letters, we wrote to look under that letter, in which there is no difficulty.

Third: In this Second Volume, under some letters, we have written brief instructions regarding leap years.
You must observe those instructions only in that leap year in which those same letters are the calendar letters, and not in other years.
For example, under the calendar letter T, it is written in small cursive script:
"When a leap year occurs, and the letter of the year is 19, on this Saturday do not celebrate [Saint] Theodosius," etc.
You must observe this instruction only in that year which is a leap year and when the letter of the year is 19.
But in other years, whether leap year or not, celebrate without hesitation just as is set down under each respective letter.
Keep this same rule for all letters where such instructions are written concerning the leap letters.

Fourth: At the beginning of whichever table the daily tones are not placed,
you must maintain the order of the tones from the day of the Eve of Theophany [Birth],
and the [hymns of the] Fathers [Harts] of the Martyrs and of the Fasts, and the Ascension [Hambartsi] hymns of the Resurrection,
up to the Sunday of Real Good Friday [Great Lent Eve].
For it was not possible to place them at the beginning of every letter.
