## English

5. Thursday. Tone 1 [AK]. Of the Holy Forefathers: Adam, Abel, Seth, Enosh, Enoch, Noah, Melchizedek, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph, Moses, Aaron, Eleazar, Joshua, Samuel, Samson, Jephthah, Barak, Gideon, and the other holy forefathers.
7. Saturday. Tone 4 [BK]. Of the sons and grandsons of Saint Gregory the Illuminator: Aristakes, Vrtanes, Husik, Grigoris, and Daniel.
8. Sunday. Tone 5 [GC]. Fourth [Sunday after Theophany]. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 6 [GK]: "Today Christ is sacrificed for us" (Aysor zenav mez patarag). Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi], Tone 7 [GY]: "With the awesome voice of resurrection" (I dzayn ahegh yarutean) [including "The angel"].
9. Monday. Tone 6 [GK]. Of the Maccabees: Eleazar the priest, Shamuna, and her seven sons. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 4 [BK]: "We bless You" (Orhnemk zkez).
10. Tuesday. Tone 5 [GC]. Of the twelve prophets: Hosea, Amos, Micah, Joel, Obadiah, Jonah, Nahum, Habakkuk, Zephaniah, Haggai, Zechariah, and Malachi.
11. Wednesday. Tone 8 [DK]. Canticle of the Fathers of repentance, Tone 4 [BK]: "Blessed art You, Lord, God of our fathers" (Harts-n merots orhneal es ter).
12. Thursday. Tone 1 [AD]. Of Sophia and her three daughters: Pistis, Elpis, and Agape. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 4 [BK]: "Those who triumphed in the battle of the law" (Ork i marti norinats-n yaghtets-in).
14. Saturday. Tone 3 [BD]. Presentation of our Lord to the Temple [Tiarnndaraj].
15. Sunday. Tone 4 [BK]. Fifth [Sunday]. Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi], Tone 4 [BK]: "The resurrection of Christ" (Zyarutiun Kriskosi). Eve of the Fast of Catechumens [Arachavorats].
16. Monday. Tone 5 [GD]. Fast of Catechumens [Arachavorats]. Canticle of the Fathers of repentance, Tone 4 [BK]: "Lord God of our fathers" (Ter astuats harts-n merots).
22. Sunday. Tone 3 [BD]. Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi], Tone 3 [BD]: "The marvelous women" (Skancheli kanayk-n).
28. Saturday. Tone 1 [AD]. Of Sahak the Parthian. From here onward, look under letter 'P' [Փ] in the calendar directory, for the letter 'K' [Ք] directory is only for leap years and is used until the end of February.
29. Sunday. Tone 2 [AK]. Mid-Fast. Canticle of the Fathers, Tone 8 [DK]: "Who sittest upon the awesome throne" (Or yaheghakerp ator nstis). Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi], Tone 2 [AK]: "We shall sing to You with the angels" (Yergests-uk kez end hresh...).
7. Sunday. Tone 8 [DK]. Great Eve of Lent [Bun Barekendan].
