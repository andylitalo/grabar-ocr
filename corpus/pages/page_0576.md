August

Saturday. Tone 6 [GK]. Of the Holy Patriarchs: Athanasius and Cyril.

Sunday. Tone 1 [AH]. Fifth [Sunday after Pentecost]. Canticle Father [Harts] Tone 6 [Gz]: Glorified God. [Hambartsi] Tone 2 [Bk]: Anticipating [the women].

Monday. Of Eugenios, Macarius, Valerius, Candidus, and Aquilas. Canticle Father [Harts] Tone 8 [Dk]: Who with miraculous power.

Tuesday. Of Andrew the Commander, and his army, and Callinicus, and Diomedes, and [the other witnesses].

Thursday. Of Adrianus, and his wife Anatolia, and of the martyrs Thalelaeus and Cosmas. Canticle Father [Harts] Tone 1 [Ah]: We bless You, righteous.

Saturday. Of the Holy Council of Ephesus, of the two hundred Patriarchs.

Sunday. Tone 2 [BK]. Sixth [Sunday after Pentecost]. Canticle Father [Harts] Tone 4 [Dg]: We glorify You. [Hambartsi] Tone 6 [Ez]: The divine [tomb]. Fast-breaking [Barikendan] of the Fast of the Holy Mother of God.

Monday. Fast of the Holy Mother of God. Canticle Father [Harts] of Fasting Tone 1 [Ah] (or Tone 2 [Ap]): Who awesome.

Sunday. Assumption of the Holy Mother of God. The intervening meat-eating period is three weeks. See in the First Volume, keeping the order of this place.

Sunday. Tone 2 [AK]. Fourth [Sunday after the Assumption]. Canticle Father [Harts] Tone 6 [Gk]: Ineffable. [Hambartsi] Tone 7 [Dz]: The risen Lord. Fast-breaking [Barikendan] of the Fast of the Holy Cross.

Monday. Tone 3 [BDz]. Fast. Feast of the Nativity of the Holy Mother of God.

Tuesday. Tone 4 [BK]. Of the Fast. Canticle Father [Harts] of Fasting Tone 4 [Bk]: [We raise] the voice of the youths.

Sunday. Tone 1 [ADz]. Exaltation of the Holy Cross. See the 14th Sunday in the First Volume, and keep the order for the feast and the fast. And for the Epistles and the daily lectionary readings and the calendar cycle, keep the order.
