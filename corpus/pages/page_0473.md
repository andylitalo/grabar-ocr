"Who are these, that I may open? For this is the door of the Lord, and the righteous enter through it."
And the priest from without shall say:
"Not only do the righteous enter, but also sinners, justified through confession and repentance, enter through it."
From within, he shall say:
"For this is the door of heaven and the valley of sorrow, which God covenanted to Jacob; rest for the righteous, expiation for sinners, the royal palace of Christ, the dwelling place of angels, the assembly of the saints, the place of refuge and house of God."
The Priest from without:
"True and worthy are those things you say concerning this holy church; for she is our immaculate mother, and from her we are born as children of light and truth; and she is our hope of life, and through her we find salvation of souls; and because she is our path of righteousness, through her we ascend to Christ our heavenly Father."
And they shall say the hymn [Sharakan] tone 2nd plagal [Bki - բկ]: "Wise Virgins" (Imastun Kusankn). "My Hope" (Hoys im). "My Sins" (Meghq im).
And at the completion, the priest from without shall begin:
"Open to us, O Lord, open to us, O Lord, open to us, O Lord, the door of mercy, we who call upon You with lamentation."
From within, he shall say:
"Behold, we see our Lord Christ, who is seated upon the holy altar, and calls to Himself His willing ones, saying: 'Where I am, there also my servant shall be.'"
The Priest from without:
"We are the price of the incorruptible blood of the Son of God; with joy let us bless Christ our Savior, and let us all say: Glory to Your awesome coming, O Lord."
Hymn [Sharakan] tone 2nd class [B3 - բձ]: "King of Glory, to You."
The Priest from without:
"Open to us, O Lord, open to us, O Lord, open to us, O Lord, the door of mercy, we who call upon You with lamentation."
From within:
"The herald of the awesome God cries out with a formidable voice, saying: 'Behold, the bridegroom comes, go out to meet him.'"
And the priest from without, with fear and trembling, shall say:
"Behold, I and my children whom God gave to me, we all stand and await the sight of Christ, and to hear His blessed voice."
From within:
"The voice of God the Creator cries out, saying: 'Come, blessed of My Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the beginning of the world.'"
And then the priest from without shall say:
"Open to me the gates of righteousness, that I may enter through them and give thanks [to the Lord]."
And they shall open the door, and all the people shall enter into the church with spiritual joy.
And they shall begin the hymn [Sharakan] tone 3rd plagal [Gki - գկ]: "Open to us the door of Your mercy." And then: "Orthos" [Stand].
Gospel: John 12:12 "The next day..." to verse 23 "...that the Son of Man may be glorified." Read this Gospel on the bim [altar stage].
Litany [Qaroz]: "Let us ask..." "Save [us]." Prayer: "King of Peace."

Great Monday. Hymn tone 2nd class [B3 - բձ]: "Uncreated from the Father," with its canons.
Morning song: "From the beginning, Lord." Response [Phokh]: "They shall perish."
Litany [Qaroz]: "Let us glorify." "Holy God," [with the clause] "who came and art to come."
After the Canticle of the Youths [Mankunq], Litany [Qaroz]: "For peace." Prayer: "Holy art Thou, Lord."
After that, Psalm 103 [104], in a mourning tone: "Confession and majesty..." Response [Phokh]: "Bless the Lord, O my soul."
Gospel: Matthew 21:18 "And in the morning, as..." to verse 22:14 "...and few are chosen."
And say the "Creator of All" [Ararchakan] as appropriate.
