## English

Jonah 4:11 "and much cattle."
And then: "For the Souls" [Requiem].
Again, know this: that when the letter of the year is "Alis" [letter Ա], the eight-day octave of the Nativity of Christ takes two days from the fast. And if the letter of the year is "E" [letter Ը], it takes one day from the fast, during which, although the feast of the Nativity is celebrated with services and Liturgy, yet the fast is unbreakable. Furthermore, from here until the Great Barekendan [Pre-Lenten Sunday], although feasts are set as immovable, yet you must not let the annual calendar out of your hands.

Saturday: of the holy saints Sarkis the General, and his son Martiros, and his fourteen soldiers. Tone 1 [ah, ak]: "To the All-Holy Trinity" (Amenasurp errorduteann). Father of the Tone [Harts] Tone 6 [gk]: "The Soldiership" (Zinvorutiunk). Magnificat [Mankunk] Tone 1 [ad]: "The Soldier of Christ" (Zinavorn kristosi). Midday Entrance Hymn: "Soldier" (Zorakan).
Psalm 5: "Lord, as with a shield" [Psalm 5:12], Antiphon: "To my words" [Psalm 5:1].
Proverbs 3:13: "Blessed is the man..." to verse 17: "with peace."
Prophet Isaiah 41:1: "Keep silence before me..." to verse 3: "lest he should come."
Epistle to the Ephesians 6:10: "Finally, be strong..." to verse 17: "the word of God."
Alleluia: Of the Martyrs, according to the tone.
Gospel according to Luke 21:10: "Then said he unto them..." to verse 19: "your souls."

Sunday. Father of the Tone [Harts] Tone 8 [dk]: "You, the Word without beginning" (Anskizbn band).
Magnificat [Metsatsustse] Tone 8 [dk]: "Glorious Mother of God" (Astvadsatsin paytsar).
Midday Psalm 64 [65]: "Praise waiteth for thee" (Kez vayele).
Prophet Isaiah 63:7: "I will mention the lovingkindnesses..." to verse 18: "of thy holiness."
Epistle, 2 Timothy 3:1: "This know also..." to verse 12: "shall suffer persecution."
Alleluia: Of the Resurrection.
Gospel according to John 6:22: "The day following..." to verse 38: "that sent me."

Know here: that after the first Sunday of the Nativity, the Sunday of the Barekendan of the Fast of Catechumens [Arachavorats], and for this Sunday, the Father of the Tone [Harts] hymns, which are: Tone 4 [dg]: "The Dawning of the Only-Begotten" (Tsagumn miadsni); Tone 5 [gdz]: "With Glory" (Paravorelov); and Tone 8 [dk]: "You, the Word without beginning" (Anskizbn band)—which were placed as immovable—yet their immobility lasts from the 20th letter of the year until the 36th [K] letter of the year. But from the 1st letter of the year to the 10th letter of the year, this rule is not kept. Therefore, in such years, you must look at the letter of the year, and according to their indication, say these Father of the Tone [Harts] hymns of these three Sundays.

Magnificat [Mankunk] and Dismissal [Hambartsi], Tone 3 [bdz]: "Verily in the place" (Stuguteamp teghev).

Gospel according to Luke 12:4: "But I say unto you..." to verse 8: "before the angels of God."
