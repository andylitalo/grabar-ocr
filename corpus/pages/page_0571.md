Fast-Entrance for the Fast of Elijah.

July

4. Sunday, Tone 2 [BDZ]. Vardavar [Transfiguration]. The period of non-fasting is five weeks. See the first volume up to Friday of the second week, and on Saturday come here, and celebrate these feasts which are after the Octave.

24. Saturday, Tone 4 [DZDZ]. Anthony the Hermit.

25. Sunday, Tone 4 [DK]. Fourth [Sunday after Vardavar]. Canticle [Harts] Tone 2 [BK]: "Who didst the heavenly host." Evening Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 4 [DK]: "When they came" and "The angel descended."

26. Monday, Tone 1 [ADZ]. King Theodosius, and the Children of Ephesus.

27. Tuesday, Tone 1 [AK]. Cyriacus and Julitta. Canticle [Harts] Tone 3 [GDZ]: "Holy art Thou, Lord of hosts."

29. Thursday, Tone 2 [BK]. Vahan of Goghtn, and Gordius, Polyeuctus, and Gregory. Canticle [Harts] Tone 4 [DK]: "To Thee, God."

31. Saturday, Tone 3 [GK]. The Patriarchs Athanasius and Cyril, and Gregory the Theologian.

August

1. Sunday, Tone 4 [DZ]. Fifth [Sunday]. Canticle [Harts] Tone 2 [BK]: "Without beginning, God." Evening Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 4 [DZDZ]: "The women anticipating."

2. Monday, Tone 4 [DK]. Eugenia the Virgin and her father, etc. Canticle [Harts] Tone 4 [DK]: "Who the wondrous."

3. Tuesday, Tone 1 [ADZ]. Tryphon, Barsamas, and Onuphrius the Hermits.

5. Thursday, Tone 2 [BDZ]. Eugenius, Macarius, etc. Canticle [Harts] Tone 1 [ADZ]: "Thee, righteous."

January

6. WEDNESDAY. Feast of the Nativity and Epiphany of Christ our God. The period of non-fasting is twelve days.

13. Wednesday. Octave of the Nativity, and the Circumcision of Christ.

14. Thursday. John the Forerunner [the Baptist].

16. Saturday. Peter the Patriarch, Blaise the Bishop, and Absalom the Deacon.

17. Sunday. Fast-Entrance for the Fast of the Catechumens. Canticle [Harts] Tone 4 [DZDZ]: "Dawning of the Only-begotten."

24. Sunday. [Feast of] Saint Sarkis. Canticle [Harts] Tone 4 [DZDZ]: "With glory." See according to the order in the first volume up to Vardavar, keeping the order of this place.

February

7. Sunday, Tone 4 [DK]. Great Lent-Entrance [Poon Barekendan].

14. Sunday, Tone 4 [DZDZ]. Second [Sunday] of Great Lent. Presentation of the Lord [Tyarnndaraj]. See according to the order in the first volume.

March
