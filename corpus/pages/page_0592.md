24. Monday. Tone 1 [ATS]. [Feast of] Julian and Vasilouhi. Canticle of the Fathers (Harts) Tone 4 [bk]: "We bless thee, Lord" (Orhnemq ezqez ter).

30. Sunday. Tone 7 [DZ]. Second [Sunday of Advent]. Canticle of the Fathers (Harts) Tone 2 [ak]: "Unoriginate Word" (Anskizbn band). Lord's Prayer/Evening Hymn (Hambartsi) Tone 7 [dz]: "Who art above" (Or i verd es). See also the others in the First Volume.

7. Sunday. Tone 6 [GK]. Third [Sunday of Advent]. Canticle of the Fathers (Harts) Tone 5 [bz]: "Who art the King" (Or t'agavord es). Lord's Prayer/Evening Hymn (Hambartsi) Tone 6 [gk]: "Taking and our" (Areal ev mer). Fast-break (Barekendan) of the Fast of Saint James.

9. Tuesday. Tone 8 [DK]. Fast. Feast of the Conception of the Holy Virgin by Anna.

23. Tuesday. Tone 6 [GK]. [Feast of] David the Prophet and James the Apostle.

28. Sunday. Tone 3 [BZ]. Sixth [Sunday of Advent]. Canticle of the Fathers (Harts) Tone 6 [gk]: "Before eternity" (Nakhahavityan). Lord's Prayer/Evening Hymn (Hambartsi) Tone 3 [bz]: "Today they rejoiced" (Aysor zvart'ats'an).

29. Monday. Tone 4 [BK]. [Feast of the] Sons of Thunder [James and John]. Fast-break (Barekendan) of the Nativity Fast.

30. Tuesday. Tone 5 [GZ]. Fast of the Nativity of Christ. Canticle of the Fathers (Harts) Tone 6 [gk]: "Blessed Lord God, blessed" (Orhneal ter astvats orhneal). And see the others in order.
