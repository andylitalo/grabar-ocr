2. Sunday, Tone 4 [BK / Tone 2 Plagal]. Fourth. Canticle of the Fathers [Harst] Tone 3 [GD / Tone 2]: "We glorify You" (Paravoremk zkez). Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 4 [BK]: "Christ is risen" (Kristos yaryav).

3. Monday, Tone 5 [GD / Tone 3]. Saint Andrew the General and his army, etc.

4. Tuesday, Tone 6 [GK / Tone 3 Plagal]. Saint Adrian and his wife Anatolia, etc. Canticle of the Fathers [Harst] Tone 6 [GK]: "To You, O God" (Ar kez astvats).

5. Wednesday, Tone 7 [DZ / Tone 4]. Canticle of the Fathers [Harst] of Repentance Tone 4 [BK]: "O God of our fathers" (Vor hartsyn merots astvats).

6. Thursday, Tone 8 [DK / Tone 4 Plagal]. Saints Abraham and Khoren, Cosmas and Damian, and the martyr Astvatsatur. Canticle of the Fathers [Harst] Tone 8 [DK]: "Who with wondrous" (Vor zhrashapar).

8. Saturday, Tone 2 [AK / Tone 1 Plagal]. Feast of the Nativity of the Holy Mother of God.

9. Sunday, Tone 3 [BD / Tone 2]. Fifth. Canticle of the Fathers [Harst] Tone 6 [GK]: "Ineffable" (Anchareli). Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 3 [BD]: "At the grave" (I gerezman) and "They came to the tomb" (Ekin i gerezman). Carnival [Barekendan] of the Fast of the Holy Cross.

10. Monday, Tone 4 [BK / Tone 2 Plagal]. Fast of the Holy Cross. Canticle of the Fathers [Harst] of Repentance Tone 4 [BK]: "Lord God of our fathers" (Ter astvats hartsyn merots).

16. Sunday, Tone 2 [AK / Tone 1 Plagal]. Exaltation of the Holy Cross. Up to the tenth Sunday, see in order the First Volume, keeping the order of this place.

23. Sunday, Tone 1 [AD / Tone 1]. Second [after Cross]. Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 1 [AD]: "With the women" (Ynt kanaysn). Carnival [Barekendan] of the Holy Cross of Varag.

24. Monday, Tone 2 [AK / Tone 1 Plagal]. Fast. Saint Mamas, etc. Canticle of the Fathers [Harst] Tone 1 [AD]: "You, the righteous" (Yzkez ardar).

26. Wednesday, Tone 4 [BK / Tone 2 Plagal]. Canticle of the Fathers [Harst] of Repentance Tone 5 [GD]: "Blessed and glorified art You, O Lord" (Orhneal es ter ev paravoreal).

30. Sunday, Tone 8 [DK / Tone 4 Plagal]. Third [after Cross]. Feast of the Holy Cross of Varag. The period of non-fasting is seven weeks. See the order in the First Volume.

7. Sunday, Tone 7 [DZ / Tone 4]. Fourth. Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 7 [DZ]: "The women anticipating" (Kanantsn kanchelov) and "The awesome" (Ahagin).

14. Sunday, Tone 6 [GK / Tone 3 Plagal]. Fifth. Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 6 [GK]: "The divine" (Zastvatsayin) and "Your resurrection" (Yzkoy harutyund).

21. Sunday, Tone 5 [GD / Tone 3]. Sixth. Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 5 [GD]: "The oil-bearers" (Yughaber) and "Today the resurrection" (Aysor harutyun) and "In the sign of the cross" (I khachanish).
