## English

He shall sign the bunch of grapes with the cross three times, and then bless [them].

In the Evening
At the tenth hour, they shall assemble in the church, and they shall begin Psalm 131 [132] with the refrain: "Arise, O Lord." Verse: "Remember, O Lord, David."
The Scripture citation is from the morning service. Alleluia: "The Holy Spirit shall come." Gospel according to Luke 1:39: "Mary arose," to verse 56: "to her house."
And they shall go out in procession with the cross and the Gospel, singing the Hymn Tone 4 [GY - դՅ]: "Today assembled." After this, Psalm 4: "There was signed." Gospel according to John 19:25: "And there stood by the cross," to verse 30: "He yielded up the spirit." And they shall bless the four corners of the world [Andastan]. Litany: "By the Holy Cross." Prayer: "Guardian."
And then: "Incline" and "Save." Messedi of the Nativity: "The Lord said to me." Verse: "Why did they rage?" Litany: "Holy Mother." Evening Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 8 [DK - դկ]: "Today ineffable," say both together.
After this: "For peace." Prayer: "Merciful Father." And they shall vest together and go out into the chancel. Hymn Tone 8 [DK - դկ]: "Who of Your only-begotten Son," with its canon [sar]. Litany: "The Holy Mother of God." "Save." Prayer: "Receive, Lord."

On the Second Day, in the Morning
Morning Hymn [AH - ահ] Tone 8 [DK - դկ]: "Today ineffable," say "The East" [Arevelk] together. Paten/Father [Harts] Tone 8 [DK - դկ]: "Who of the only-begotten," with its canon.
Morning Song: "He shall come down like rain." Litany: "Holy Mother." Psalm 71 [72] with the refrain: "He shall come down." Verse: "God, Your judgment." Gospel according to Luke 2:1: "And it came to pass in those days," to verse 7: "place in the inn." Song: "Creator-like." Litany: "Let us ask." "Save." Prayer: "To Your almighty."

At the Midday Liturgy
Introit: "O Mother of God, confessing you." Hymn Tone 8 [DK - դկ]: "Mother of God, luminous cloud." Psalm 71 [72]: "He shall come down like rain." Verse: "God, Your judgment."
Proverbs 11:30: "From the fruit of righteousness," to 12:4: "is a crown to her husband."
Prophet Zechariah 2:10: "Sing and rejoice," to verse 13: "of His holy ones."
Epistle of the Apostle 2 Corinthians 6:16: "For you are the temple of God," to 7:1: "in the fear of God."
Alleluia: "Rejoice, O favored one."
Gospel according to Luke 1:39: "Mary arose," to verse 56: "to her house."

In the Evening

And after "Merciful Father," they shall vest together, and they shall begin the Hymn Tone 7 [DZ - դձ]: "Who the sentence of labor." Litany: "The Holy Mother of God." "Save." Prayer: "Receive, Lord."
