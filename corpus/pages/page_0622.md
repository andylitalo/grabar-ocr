## English

SEVENTH WEEK OF THE ROMANS [Julian Calendar / Latin Section]

In February:

1. Saturday, Tone 2 [BK]. Gregory the Theologian.
2. Sunday, Tone 3 [GCh/GZ]. Third [Sunday]. Canticle [Harts] Tone 6 [GK]: "Today He who was sacrificed" (Aysor zenaw). Dismissal [Hambartsi] Tone 5 [GDz/G3]: "With a terrible voice" (I dzayn ahegh). For what is placed below, wait one week so that you may look up Transfiguration [Vardavar] in the First Volume.
3. Monday, Tone 6 [GK]. The Maccabees, Eleazar, and the others. Canticle [Harts] Tone 3 [BDz/B3]: "With Your saints" (Ynd sourbs k’o).
4. Tuesday, Tone 7 [DDz/DCh]. The twelve prophets: Hosea, and the others.
6. Thursday, Tone 1 [ADz/ADz]. Sophia and her three daughters. Canticle [Harts] Tone 4 [BK]: "We bless You" (Orhnemk’ zk’ez).
8. Saturday, Tone 3 [BDz]. The sons and grandsons of the Enlightener.
9. Sunday, Tone 4 [BK]. Fourth [Sunday]. Dismissal [Hambartsi] Tone 4 [BK]: "The Resurrection" (Yzharout’iwn). Eve of the Fast of Catechumens [Arachavorats].
10. Monday, Tone 5 [GDz/GZ]. Fast of Catechumens. Canticle of Penitence [Harts apashkharut’ean] Tone 4 [BK]: "The voice of the youths" (Mankantsn zdzayn).
13. Thursday, Tone 8 [DK]. Commemoration of Jonah the Prophet.
14. Friday, Tone 1 [ADz/AZ]. Fast. Presentation of the Lord [Tyarnndarach].

In March:

2. Sunday, Tone 8 [DK]. Great Lent Eve [Boun Barekendan]. See also the others according to their order in the First Volume, finding the order of this place.

In April:

7. Monday, Tone 4 [BK]. Feast of the Annunciation of the Holy Virgin.
20. Sunday, Tone 1 [ADz/AZ]. Easter: the day of the Resurrection of Christ. See according to the order in the First Volume.

In January:

6. MONDAY, Tone 2 [AK]. Feast of the Nativity and Epiphany of Christ. The period of non-fasting is thirty-five days.
14. Tuesday, Tone 2 [AK]. John the Forerunner [Karapet].
15. Wednesday, Tone 3 [BDz]. Canticle of Penitence [Harts apashkharut’ean] Tone 3 [BDz]: "Who saved" (Or apresouts`er).
16. Thursday, Tone 4 [BK]. Peter the Patriarch, Blaise [Vlas], and the others.
18. Saturday, Tone 6 [GK]. Anton, and Tryphon, Barsama, and Onuphrius the hermits.
19. Sunday, Tone 7 [DDz]. First [Sunday]. Dismissal [Hambartsi] Tone 7 [DDz/D3]: "The Resurrection of the Son of God" (Yzharout’iwn astouatsordvoyn), to the end.
20. Monday, Tone 8 [DK]. Theodosius the King, and the Holy Children of Ephesus.
21. Tuesday, Tone 1 [ADz/AZ]. Cyriacus and Julitta, and Gordius, Polyeuctus, and Grigoris. Canticle [Harts] Tone 1 [ADz]: "We justify You" (Yzke’z ardar).
23. Thursday, Tone 3 [BDz]. Vahan of Goghtn, and Eugenia the Virgin, and the others. Canticle [Harts] Tone 2 [AK]: "Holy God, the Creator" (Sourb astouats ararichn).
25. Saturday, Tone 5 [GDz/GZ]. Athanasius and Cyril, Patriarchs.
26. Sunday, Tone 6 [GK]. Second [Sunday]. Canticle [Harts] Tone 5 [GDz/G3]: "Creator of all creatures" (Ararichd tararatsots). Dismissal [Hambartsi] Tone 6 [GK]: "A lightning-like vision" (Tesil p’aylaknadzev).
27. Monday, Tone 7 [DDz]. Eugenius, Macarius, and the others. Canticle [Harts] Tone 2 [AK]: "O Most High" (Or bardzreald).
28. Tuesday, Tone 8 [DK]. Andrew the General, and his army, and the others.
30. Thursday, Tone 2 [AK]. Adrianus, and his wife Anatolia, and the others. Canticle [Harts] Tone 3 [BDz/B3]: "Forgiving" (Anokhakal).
