CANON
OF THE DORMITION [LITERALLY: TRANSLATION] OF THE HOLY MOTHER OF GOD
ON THE EVE, AND NIGHT
There is a vigil. They shall place the Holy Relics [or Sacrament] upon the cushion in the chancel.
Processional hymn, Tone 8 [d-k]: The East from the highest [Arevelk gerarpin].
And the priests shall vest together and come into the chancel,
And they shall begin: Canticle [of the Dawn], Tone 5 [g-3]: Today gathered [Aysor zhoghovyal] of the saints.
Alleluia of the Nativity.
Patristic Hymn [Harts], Tone 7 [d-dz]: Who [concerning] the first mother [Vor znaghamorn].
Morning Song: Arise, O Lord, into Thy rest [Ari ter i hangist ko].
Litanist: Holy Mother [Mayr surb].
Holy God, Who came to the translation of Your mother and virgin.
And they shall go outside with the Cross and Gospel and all ornaments in procession, singing the hymn:
Tone 5 [g-3] [originally written g-d-y-dz / g-3]: Today gathered [Aysor zhoghovyal].
Afterward: Psalm 131 [132]: Arise, O Lord, into Thy rest. Antiphon: Remember, O Lord, David.
Scripture: Song of Songs 4:9: You have ravished my heart... to verse 15: from Lebanon; 8:14 [with additions]: The Lord made you... to verse 8:14: unblemished. This is a compilation.

Litany: For the peace from above [Vasni verust].
And they shall sign the four corners of the world [Andastan].
Afterward: By Your Holy Cross [Surb khachivs]. Prayer: Guard us [Pahpanea].
And then, hymn Tone 8 [d-k]: Only-begotten Jesus [Yisus miadzin].

Prayer: To Your all-powerful and wondrous Coming.
