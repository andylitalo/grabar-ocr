## English

In July:
2. Sunday. Tone 2 [BK - B2]. Transfiguration (Vartavar). The post-feast period is 13 [days].
It is four weeks. Until the Friday of the third
week, see Volume One, and on Saturday come here, and
perform the feasts set below, which
are after the Octave of the Nativity and after
the Assumption [of the Mother of God].

August:
1. Saturday. Tone 7 [DZ - D2]. Of the Holy Patriarchs Athanasius
and Cyril, and Gregory the
Theologian.
2. Sunday. Tone 8 [DK - D4]. Fourth [Sunday after Pentecost]. Canticle [Harts] Tone 4 [BK - B4]: "Who with the heavenly hosts" (Or yergnayin zin.). Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 8 [DK - D4]: "When they came" (Yorzham yegin) and "The angel descended" (Yev ej hreshtagn).
3. Monday. Tone 1 [AJ - A2]. Of Eugenia the Virgin, and others.
And of the Witnesses: Eugenius,
Macarius, etc. Canticle [Harts] Tone 7 [DZ - D2]: "Holy
art Thou, Lord of hosts" (Surp es der zorutyants).
4. Tuesday. Tone 2 [AK - A4]. Of Andreas the Commander, and
his army, etc.
6. Thursday. Tone 4 [BK - B4]. Of Adrianus and his wife, etc.
Canticle [Harts] Tone 6 [GK - G4]: "To Thee, O God" (Ar kez astuadz).
8. Saturday. Tone 6 [GK - G4]. Of the Holy Council of Ephesus,
the two hundred Patriarchs.
9. Sunday. Tone 7 [DZ - D2]. Fifth [Sunday after Pentecost]. Canticle [Harts] Tone 8 [DK - D4]:
"We glorify Thee" (Paravoremk zkez). Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 5 [GD - G2]:
"Anticipating with the women" (Gantsantsn ganhelov). Fast-break [Baregentan]
of the Fast of the Mother of God.
10. Monday. Tone 8 [DK - D4]. Fast of the Holy Mother of
God. Canticle [Harts] of Repentance Tone 3 [BD - B2]: "Who saved" (Or abretsu).
16. Sunday. Tone 8 [DK - D4]. Assumption of the Holy Mother of
God. The post-feast period is three
weeks. See Volume One,
preserving the order of this place.

September:
6. Sunday. Tone 3 [BD - B2]. Fourth [Sunday]. Canticle [Harts] Tone 6 [GK - G4]: "Ineffable" (Andshareli). Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 3 [BD - B2]: "At the tomb" (I kerezman)
and "They came to the tomb" (Yev yegin i gerez). Fast-break [Baregentan]
of the Fast of the Holy Cross.
7. Monday. Tone 4 [BK - B4]. Fast of the Holy Cross. Canticle [Harts]
of Repentance Tone 4 [BK - B4]: "Lord God of our
fathers" (Der astuadz harts nmerots).
Tuesday. Tone 5 [GD - G2]. Fast. Feast of the Birth of the Holy
Virgin from Anna.
Sunday. Tone 2 [AK - A4]. Exaltation of the Holy Cross. See Volume One,
up to the tenth Sunday,
preserving the order of this place.
Sunday. Tone 1 [AJ - A2]. Second [Sunday after the Cross]. Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 1 [AD - A2]: "With the women" (Ynt ganaysn). Fast-break [Baregentan] of the Cross of Varag.
Monday. Tone 2 [AK - A4]. Fast. Of Mamas, etc.
Canticle [Harts] Tone 1 [AD - A2]: "Thee, the righteous" (Yzkez artar).
Wednesday. Tone 4 [BK - B4]. Canticle [Harts] Tone 5 [GD - G2]: "Glorified is
Thy name forever" (Paravoryal e anun ko yavityan).
Sunday. Tone 8 [DK - D4]. Third [Sunday]. Feast of the Holy Cross of Varag. The post-feast period is seven
weeks. See in order in Volume
One.

October:
Sunday. Tone 7 [DZ - D2]. Fourth [Sunday]. Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 7 [DZ - D2]:
"Awesome" (Ahakin).
Sunday. Tone 6 [GK - G4]. Fifth [Sunday]. Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 5 [GD - G2]:
"The Divine" (Zastuadzayin) and "Thy Resurrection" (Yzko yarutyunt).
Sunday. Tone 5 [GD - G2]. Sixth [Sunday]. Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 5 [GD - G2]: "The Myrrh-bearing women" (Yukhaper) and "Today resurrection" (Aysor yarutyun) and
"By the Sign of the Cross" (I Khachanish).
Sunday. Tone 4 [BK - B4]. Seventh [Sunday]. Discovery of the Cross.
Tuesday. Tone 2 [AK - A4]. Of the Hyperperechians, and of Severian of Sebaste, etc.
Canticle [Harts] Tone 8 [DK - D4]: "Who [adorned] wondrously" (Or zhrashapar).
Wednesday. Tone 7 [DZ - D2]. Canticle [Harts] Tone 6 [GK - G4]: "Holy
God, Father" (Surp astuadz hayr).
Saturday. Tone 2 [AK - A4]. Of All Saints, old and
new, etc. See also the others in Volume
One, preserving the order of this place.

November:
Sunday. Tone 3 [BD - B2]. Eighth [Sunday]. Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 3 [BD - B2]:
"The voice of good tidings" (Zayn avedavor).
Sunday. Tone 2 [AK - A4]. Ninth [Sunday]. Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 2 [AK - A4]:
"Risen" (Yarutsyal) and "Wondrous angel" (Skancheli hreshtag).

"Who ineffably" (Or andshar), to the end. Fast-break [Baregentan]

Tone 7 [DZ - D2]: "God of our fathers, who
