## English

On the feast of Martyrs and other common saints who do not have a proper blessing: from the Tone of the day, say the Martyrs' "The Lord in heaven" [Ter yerknits], and then one more Hymn of other saints according to the propriety of the day; and after the Coming of the Spirit, and the rest according to order.

Monday. Tone 3 [gd]: Of the holy Infants of Bethlehem, and of Akakios the witness, and of Movkima the priest, and of Kodratos the soldier. Canticle [Harts] Tone 3 [dg]: "Today the innocent holy" with its proper parts. Midday Liturgy Psalm 8: "Out of the mouths of babes". Proverbs 29:2 "In praise of" to verse 7 "righteous minds". Prophet Isaiah 18:7 "In that time" to verse 7 "Mount Zion". Apostle Hebrews 2:14 "Forasmuch then" to verse 18 "to succor". Alleluia: "The Lord loveth justice". Gospel Matthew 2:16 "Then when he saw" to verse 18 "because they were not".

Tuesday. Tone 8 [gk]: Of the holy virgins Nune and Mane. Say the Blessing, the Canticle [Harts], and all the rest of Saint Hripsime. Midday Liturgy Introit: "Betrothed of Christ". Psalm 44 [45]: "They shall be brought". Book of Song of Songs 8:14 "Flee, my beloved" to verse 24 "spotless". Prophet Isaiah 27:11 "Women who come" to verse 13 "in Jerusalem". Catholic Epistle 1 Peter 5:8 "Be sober" to verse 11 "forever, amen". Alleluia: "She shall stand". Gospel John 12:24 "Verily, verily" to verse 26 "him my Father".

Wednesday. Tone 2 [bd]: Canticle [Harts] Tone 3 [gd]: "Who co-eternal". Midday Liturgy Psalm 65 [66]: "Save Thy servants". Apostle Romans 4:13 "For not if" to verse 22 "unto righteousness". Alleluia: "My heart was troubled". Gospel Matthew 10:34 "Think not" to verse 42 "his reward".

And regarding the Martyrs' Canticles [Harts], know this:
From this Thursday, make a beginning from the first Tone to the last Tone, and say them in order; and when they end, begin again. But Tone 3 [gd] "Who boundless", Tone 8 [gk] "The military hosts", and Tone 4 [dd] "Who participated" — do not say these three Canticles in order, because they have their own proper days.

Thursday. Tone 4 [bk]: Of the holy princes Sahak and Joseph, and the witnesses Sarkis and Bakhos. Blessing [of the Tone] of the Martyrs. Canticle [Harts] Tone 1 [ad]: "Thee the righteous". Midday Liturgy Psalm 115 [116]: "Precious is", Response: "I believed". Proverbs 6:1 "My son, if thou be" to verse 5 "from the snares". Prophet Isaiah 33:5 "Holy is the Lord" to verse 8 "them among men". Apostle Romans 8:18 "For I reckon" to verse 27 "for the saints". Alleluia: "The righteous cried". Gospel Luke 21:12 "But before" to verse 19 "your souls".

Friday. Tone 1 [ad]: Canticle [Harts] of Rest Tone 3 [gd]: "Who art the King". Midday Liturgy Psalm 97 [98]: "Have seen all". Apostle Romans 5:12 "For as by one" to verse 21 "our Lord". Alleluia: "For Thy name's sake". Gospel Matthew 11:25 "At that time" to verse 30 "faint-hearted".

Saturday. Tone 8 [gk]: Of the holy ones Nerses the Great, our patriarch, and Khat the bishop. Blessing Tone 4 [bk]: "Who the unutterable light". Canticle [Harts] Tone 3 [gd]: "Today the church celebrates" with its proper parts. Midday Liturgy Psalm 131 [132]: "Priests". Wisdom of Solomon 2:23 "For God" to 3:8 "forever". Prophet Isaiah 57:15 "Thus saith the high" to verse 16 "I made". Apostle Hebrews 13:7 "Remember your leaders" to verse 9 "so walked". Alleluia: "Thy priests". Gospel Matthew 10:16 "Behold, I" to verse 22 "he shall stand". In the Evening: "Joyful Light", and the rest according to order.

Fourth Sunday after the Coming of the Spirit. Tone 4 [dd]: Canticle [Harts] Tone 1 [ad]: "Be Thou brightened". Magnificat [Metsatsustse] Tone 1 [ad]: "Who wast chosen from the beginning". Midday Liturgy Psalm 64 [65]: "To Thee is due". Prophet Isaiah 1:1 "Hear, O heaven" to verse 15 "are full of blood". Psalm: "Holy is Thy temple". Apostle Romans 6:12 "Let not therefore" to verse 23 "in our Lord". Alleluia: "Thou hast been pleased, Lord". Gospel Matthew 12:1 "At that time went" to verse 8 "Son of man". In the Evening, Messedi: "Let Thy hand be", "Who shepherdest". Dismissal Hymn [Hambartsi] Tone 4 [dd]: "Glorifier". Proclamation: "Let us ask", "Save us". Prayer: "King of peace". And then the Fast of Saint Gregory our Illuminator; but there are feasts.
