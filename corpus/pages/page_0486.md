Dismissal Psalm 92 [93]: "The Lord reigns." Gospel John 21:1 "After these things" to verse 14 "risen from the dead."

On Saturday
Commemoration of the Beheading of Saint John the Baptist. Tone 4 [gd]: "Co-glorifier." Fathers [Hymn] Tone 1 [ad]: "From eternity," with its canon, and everything is of the Resurrection.
In the Morning
Psalm 67 [68]: "God is glorified," response: "Thy mercies." Proverbs 8:4 "To you [O men] I call" to verse 11 "cannot compare with her." Prophet Isaiah 40:3 "The voice of one crying" to verse 8 "stands forever." Prophet Malachi 4:4 "Thus says the Lord" to verse 6 "and judgment." Acts 13:26 "And as [John] fulfilled" to verse 33 "having raised Jesus." Alleluia: "You, child, [shall be called] the prophet." Gospel Mark 6:14 "And [King Herod] heard" to verse 29 "and laid it in a tomb." Litany: "Let us ask," "Save [us]." Prayer: "Your all-powerful."

At the Midday Liturgy
Introit: "Christ is risen." Psalm 19 [20]: "May He hear you," "The Lord in Your strength," "My God, my God." Acts 5:12 "And by the hands of the apostles" to verse 33 "to kill them." Psalm 66 [67]: "God be merciful." Catholic [Epistle] James 2:14 "What does it profit" to verse 26 "is dead." Alleluia, Psalm 65 [66]: "Make a joyful noise unto God," complete. Gospel John 21:15 "And when they had dined" to verse 25 "if they were written."

In the Evening
First Psalm: "God be merciful unto us," and for the dismissal: "Make a joyful noise unto God." Until the Ascension, the Saturday evening psalms are these. Gospel John 21:1 "After these things" to verse 14 "risen from the dead." "Gladsome Light." Messedi [Gradual] of the Coming: "Blessed is He who comes," "The Lord our God." "Holy God," who came and are to come. Dismissal Hymn Tone 1 [ad]: "Who are praised on high." Dismissal Psalm 99 [100]: "Make a joyful noise unto God." Gospel John 21:15 "And when they had dined" to verse 25 "if they were written." Litany: "Let us ask," "Save [us]." Prayer: "To Your all-powerful and wondrous coming."

On the Feast of the Holy Virgin Mary.
The Feast of the Annunciation of the Mother of God is placed here, which is celebrated on April 7. But in the evening, which is April 6, on whatever day it falls, after the proper [service] of the day, do the pre-feast service. Hymn Tone 2 [ak]: "The Ineffable Mystery," with its canon. After, Litany: "The Holy Mother of God." Prayer: "Receive, Lord."

And in the night, which is April 7, the priests shall vest together and come to the chancel, and begin the Morning Hymn Tone 2 [ak]: "The Ineffable Mystery," with its canon. Alleluia: of the Birth. Morning Song: "He shall come down like rain." Litany: "Holy Mother." "Holy God," according to the order of the day. Then Psalm 67 [68]: "The Lord gives the word," response: "Let God arise." Scripture: Song of Solomon 1:1 "Let him kiss me" to verse 11 "shall receive you." Proverbs 11:30 "From the fruit of righteousness," [Proverbs] 12:4 "[A virtuous woman] is a crown to her husband." Prophet Isaiah 62:7 "Thus says the Lord" to verse 10 "[salvation] of our God." Prophet Zechariah 2:10 "Sing and rejoice" to verse 13 "of His saints." Prophet Malachi 3:1 "Thus says the Lord" to verse 2 "of His appearance." Apostle 2 Corinthians 6:16 "For you [are the temple]" to 7:1 "[perfecting holiness] in the fear of God." Alleluia: "The Holy Spirit shall come." Gospel Luke 1:26 "And in the [sixth] month" to verse 38 "the angel [departed] from her." Litany: "Let us ask," "Save [us]." Prayer: "Your all-powerful." And if it falls on a Sunday of Lent, say the two stanzas of "Newly-created."
