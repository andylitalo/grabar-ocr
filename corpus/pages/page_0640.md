## English

CONCERNING THE FORTHCOMING TABLE-FORM CYCLE

The table-form cycle, which is arranged on the following page, is the perpetual cycle of the Great Paschal Cycle [Great Era / 500-year cycle] containing 532 years, which by means of the year of Christ unerringly indicates the Dominical Letter [Letter of the Year / Taregir] and the Epact [Veradir] perpetually. For upon the completion of the 532 years, it begins again from the start, only changing the date. At the beginning of the lines, the placed index numbers are the years of Christ; the topmost line contains the Epacts, and those placed in the boxes are the Dominical Letters. 

See, therefore, how it begins: it starts at the year 1909, which is placed at the beginning of the first line. Going from that year to the first box, you will find the Dominical Letter of that same year, Ը [8], and proceeding directly upward from that Dominical Letter Ը, you will find the Epact of the same year, ԺԹ [19]. And on arriving at the year 1910, going forward one box from the Dominical Letter Ը, in the same line you will find there the Dominical Letter of the year 1910, Ռ [R]. And proceeding directly upward from that Dominical Letter, you will find placed there the Epact of the same year, Լ [30]. And by this order, as much as the year increases, you go forward one box in the same line, where you will find the Dominical Letter of the year, and directly upward from the Dominical Letter, the Epact. And as you proceed in this orderly way until you reach the last box of the same line, in which your year will be 1927, you will find in the same box the Dominical Letter of that same year, Յ [Y], and directly at the head of it, the Epact Ը [8]. Then, at the end of that same year, the first line also ends, which has 19 years.

Then you must return to the beginning of the second line, in which you will find the year 1928, and opposite to it in the first box, the Dominical Letter ԽԼ [XL], and directly at the head of it, the Epact ԺԹ [19]. Keep this same instruction, which was spoken concerning the first line, in this line, and in the third, and in the fourth, and in all other lines until the end.

CONCERNING THE TABLE-FORM CYCLE

By this order, on reaching the last line, in which is the year 2422, and in its last box, in which is the Dominical Letter ՈՉ [OCh], and the year is 2440, the Great Paschal Cycle is completed, which is 532 years. For one page of your Table has 28 lines according to the cycle of the Solar Cycle [Seven-literals / Eotneryak], and each line lasts 19 years, according to the number of the 19 Epacts; and 19 times 28, or 28 times 19, is 532, which is one cycle.

For that reason, you must of necessity return here to the same first beginning line according to the rule of the cycle, and begin anew; in which your year will be 2441, and opposite to it in the first box is placed the Dominical Letter Ը [8], and at the head, the Epact ԺԹ [19], according to the first order; for you must only change the year, and proceed in an orderly way similar to the first.

But if you should wish to find the Dominical Letter of past years, you must add to the year you have, once or twice, 532 years, which is one or two cycles, and find in the forthcoming table your summed year, and the Dominical Letter of that same year is the Dominical Letter of the past year which you are seeking.

For this reason we placed the index of the cycle with this Directory [Tonatsuyts], so that having the year of whatever year at hand, by means of it you may find the Letter of the Year; and then, having found the Canon [Khoran] of the same Dominical Letter in the Second Volume, you may conduct it therewith without labor and perpetually.

Although the Epacts were not as necessary as the Dominical Letters, yet we placed them for the sake of bringing the cycle into harmony. As for the Seven-literals [Dominical Letters / Eotneryak], they are placed in the Canon of each Dominical Letter.
